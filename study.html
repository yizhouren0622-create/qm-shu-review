记号统一与适用条件Notation & assumptions

势能 V,UV,UPotential energy V,UV,U在同一模型中可作不同势能记号，量纲为能量；不要与电压 UU 混淆。Alternative potential-energy symbols in a specified model; distinguish them from voltage, which may also be denoted by UU.
遏止电压 Ua,UsU_a,U_sStopping voltage Ua,UsU_a,U_s本站使用非负的遏止电压大小，Kmax=eUaK_{\max}=eU_a，e>0e>0。U0=W/eU_0=W/e 表示逸出功对应的电压，不是红限频率。This site uses the nonnegative magnitude: Kmax=eUaK_{\max}=eU_a, e>0e>0. U0=W/eU_0=W/e is the work-function voltage, not the threshold frequency.
阱宽 a,La,LWell width a,La,L区间 (0,L)(0,L) 的总宽度为 LL；区间 (−a,a)(-a,a) 的总宽度为 2a2a。能量公式中的长度必须代入总宽度。The full width of (0,L)(0,L) is LL; the full width of (−a,a)(-a,a) is 2a2a. Use full width in the well energy formula.
质量 m,μm,\muMass m,μm,\mu本站相应课件例题中的 μ\mu 表示粒子质量；两体模型中该符号也可能表示约化质量，必须以定义为准。In the relevant slide examples, μ\mu denotes particle mass. In a two-body model it may instead denote reduced mass; check the definition.
密度 ρ,w\rho,wDensity ρ,w\rho,w归一化后 ρ=w=|Ψ|2\rho=w=|\Psi|^2（按所用维数）。密度有量纲，区间或体积积分才是无量纲概率。For normalized states, ρ=w=|Ψ|2\rho=w=|\Psi|^2 in the chosen dimension. Density has units; its interval or volume integral is a dimensionless probability.
h,ℏh,\hbarh,ℏh,\hbarℏ=h/(2π)\hbar=h/(2\pi)。当 k=2π/λk=2\pi/\lambda 时，p=ℏk=h/λp=\hbar k=h/\lambda；不能混用。ℏ=h/(2π)\hbar=h/(2\pi). With k=2π/λk=2\pi/\lambda, p=ℏk=h/λp=\hbar k=h/\lambda; keep the factor 2π2\pi.
RH,R∞R_H,R_\inftyRH,R∞R_H,R_\inftyRHR_H 对应有限核质量的氢原子；R∞R_\infty 是无限核质量极限。按题目给定的近似和单位使用。RHR_H includes finite nuclear mass for hydrogen; R∞R_\infty is the infinite-nuclear-mass limit. Follow the question’s approximation and units.
本征值 / 平均值Eigenvalue / expectation单次测量得到允许的本征值；大量相同制备测量的平均值按概率加权，未必是允许的单次结果。One measurement gives an eigenvalue. The ensemble mean is probability-weighted and need not be a possible single outcome.
厄米 / 幺正Hermitian / unitary厄米 F†=FF^\dagger=F；幺正 U†U=IU^\dagger U=I。微分算符的判断还依赖定义域与边界条件。Hermitian: F†=FF^\dagger=F. Unitary: U†U=IU^\dagger U=I. Differential-operator claims also require domains and boundary conditions.

三道变式练习Three recall exercises

阱宽加倍Double the well width

同一粒子、同一量子数 nn，无限深阱宽从 LL 变成 2L2L，能量如何变化？
For the same particle and quantum number nn, how does the energy change when the well width grows from LL to 2L2L?
提示Hint
只看 EnE_n 中长度的幂次。
Inspect the power of width in EnE_n.
参考答案与易错点Answer & common error
En∝L−2E_n\propto L^{-2}，所以新能量是原来的 1/41/4。常见错误：把宽度加倍误认为能量也加倍。
En∝L−2E_n\propto L^{-2}, so the new energy is one quarter of the old energy. A common error is assuming energy doubles with width.

平移后的实高斯A shifted real Gaussian

实归一化波函数 ψ(x)=Aexp[−(x−x0)2/(4σ2)]\psi(x)=A\exp[-(x-x_0)^2/(4\sigma^2)]，求 ⟨x⟩\langle x\rangle 与 ⟨p⟩\langle p\rangle，σ>0\sigma>0。
For the real normalized wave function ψ(x)=Aexp[−(x−x0)2/(4σ2)]\psi(x)=A\exp[-(x-x_0)^2/(4\sigma^2)], find ⟨x⟩\langle x\rangle and ⟨p⟩\langle p\rangle, with σ>0\sigma>0.
提示Hint
令 u=x−x0u=x-x_0，并对动量平均值分部积分。
Substitute u=x−x0u=x-x_0 and integrate the momentum expectation by parts.
参考答案与易错点Answer & common error
密度关于 x0x_0 对称，故 ⟨x⟩=x0\langle x\rangle=x_0；⟨p⟩=−iℏ[ψ2]−∞∞/2=0\langle p\rangle=-i\hbar[\psi^2]_{-\infty}^{\infty}/2=0。实波函数不意味着位置平均值为零。
The density is centered on x0x_0, hence ⟨x⟩=x0\langle x\rangle=x_0. Decay gives ⟨p⟩=−iℏ[ψ2]−∞∞/2=0\langle p\rangle=-i\hbar[\psi^2]_{-\infty}^{\infty}/2=0. Reality does not imply zero mean position.

相对相位与测量基Relative phase and measurement basis

|ψ⟩=(|1⟩+eiϕ|2⟩)/2|\psi\rangle=(|1\rangle+e^{i\phi}|2\rangle)/\sqrt2，|1⟩,|2⟩|1\rangle,|2\rangle 正交。在原基和 |±⟩=(|1⟩±|2⟩)/2|\pm\rangle=(|1\rangle\pm|2\rangle)/\sqrt2 基中分别求概率。
For |ψ⟩=(|1⟩+eiϕ|2⟩)/2|\psi\rangle=(|1\rangle+e^{i\phi}|2\rangle)/\sqrt2 with orthonormal |1⟩,|2⟩|1\rangle,|2\rangle, find the probabilities in the original basis and in |±⟩=(|1⟩±|2⟩)/2|\pm\rangle=(|1\rangle\pm|2\rangle)/\sqrt2.
提示Hint
先计算内积得到概率幅，再取模方。
Compute projection amplitudes before taking their squared magnitudes.
参考答案与易错点Answer & common error
原基概率恒为 1/2,1/21/2,1/2；新基 P+=(1+cosϕ)/2P_+=(1+\cos\phi)/2，P−=(1−cosϕ)/2P_-=(1-\cos\phi)/2。相对相位能改变干涉，整体相位不改变任何概率。
Original-basis probabilities stay 1/2,1/21/2,1/2. In the new basis, P+=(1+cosϕ)/2P_+=(1+\cos\phi)/2, P−=(1−cosϕ)/2P_-=(1-\cos\phi)/2. Relative phase can affect interference; a global phase cannot affect probabilities.

重复测量：概率与样本平均Repeated measurement: probabilities and sample means

每次重新制备 pY1,1+1−pY1,−1\sqrt pY_{1,1}+\sqrt{1-p}Y_{1,-1}，测量 LzL_z，结果为 ±ℏ\pm\hbar。理论均值为 (2p−1)ℏ(2p-1)\hbar。预测：样本平均是否必须是一次测量的允许值？

改变 p 会清空样本，保证统计来自同一制备。模拟样本有随机波动，不会总与理论概率完全一致。
