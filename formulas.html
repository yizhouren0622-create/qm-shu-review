洛伦兹变换Lorentz transformation

γ=11−v2/c2
x′=γ(x−vt),t′=γ(t−vxc2),y′=y, z′=z

v：S′ 相对 S 沿 x 轴运动的速度；c：真空光速；γ：洛伦兹因子。v: speed of S′ along the x axis of S; c: vacuum light speed; γ: Lorentz factor.

把同一事件的时空坐标从一个惯性系转换到另一个。Convert the spacetime coordinates of one event between inertial frames.

例题与步骤Worked example+

v = 0.6c，S 中事件为 x = 0、t = 1 μs。For v = 0.6c, an event has x = 0 and t = 1 μs in S.

  1. 先计算洛伦兹因子。First calculate the Lorentz factor.

    γ=(1−0.62)−1/2=1.25

  2. 代入同一事件的坐标，得到 S′ 中的坐标。Substitute the event coordinates to obtain its coordinates in S′.

    t′=1.25μs,x′≈−225m

两坐标系原点在 t = t′ = 0 重合。The frame origins coincide at t = t′ = 0.

由温度估计黑体辐射最强的波长。Estimate the wavelength of strongest blackbody emission from temperature.

  1. 用维恩常数除以温度。Divide Wien’s constant by the temperature.

    λmax=2.898×10−35800m≈500nm

  2. 峰值位于可见光波段。The peak lies in the visible range.

黑体辐射分布Blackbody spectral distribution

ρν=8πhν3c31ehν/(kBT)−1
hν≪kBT:ρν≈8πν2kBTc3
hν≫kBT:ρν≈8πhν3c3e−hν/(kBT)

ρν：单位体积、单位频率的能量密度；ν：频率；kB：玻尔兹曼常数。ρν: energy density per unit frequency and volume; ν: frequency; kB: Boltzmann constant.

普朗克公式描述完整频谱；低频极限为瑞利–金斯公式，高频极限为维恩形式。Planck’s law gives the full spectrum; its low-frequency limit is Rayleigh–Jeans and its high-frequency limit has the Wien form.

例题与步骤Worked example+

比较 hν = kBT 时的普朗克值与瑞利–金斯值。Compare Planck and Rayleigh–Jeans predictions at hν = kBT.

  1. 令无量纲变量 u = hν/(kBT) = 1。Set the dimensionless variable u = hν/(kBT) = 1.

    ρνPlanckρνRJ=ueu−1=1e−1≈0.582

  2. 此时瑞利–金斯公式已明显高估辐射能量密度。Rayleigh–Jeans already substantially overestimates the energy density here.

ρν 的单位为 J·m⁻³·Hz⁻¹；不要与每单位波长的分布混用。ρν has units J·m⁻³·Hz⁻¹; distinguish it from the spectrum per unit wavelength.

Kmax=hν−W0=eUs
ν0=W0h,λ0=hcW0

先判断是否超过阈值，再求光电子最大动能。Check the emission threshold, then find the maximum photoelectron kinetic energy.

例题与步骤Worked example+

  1. 光子能量约为 4.13 eV，超过逸出功。The photon energy is about 4.13 eV, above the work function.

    Eγ≈1240300eV=4.13eV

  2. 扣除逸出功；用 Kmax = eUs 求电压。Subtract the work function; use Kmax = eUs to find the voltage.

德布罗意波长de Broglie wavelength

λ=hp,p≈mv
K=eU ⟹ λ≈h2meeU

p：粒子动量；U：从静止加速电子的电压大小。p: particle momentum; U: voltage magnitude accelerating an electron from rest.

估计电子的物质波波长并判断衍射尺度。Estimate an electron’s matter-wave wavelength and the scale of diffraction.

例题与步骤Worked example+

电子由静止经过 54 V 电压加速。An electron is accelerated from rest through 54 V.

  1. 获得 54 eV 动能，使用非相对论动量。Its kinetic energy is 54 eV; use the nonrelativistic momentum.

    p=2mee(54V)

  2. 代入德布罗意关系，得到原子尺度的波长。Apply the de Broglie relation to obtain an atomic-scale wavelength.

    λ≈0.167nm

ρ(r,t)=|Ψ(r,t)|2

  1. 概率密度为常数。The probability density is constant.

    ρ(x)=1L

  2. 在左侧四分之一区域积分。Integrate over the leftmost quarter.

    P(0<x<L/4)=∫0L/4dxL=14

这是说明积分规则的理想化瞬时分布，不是无限深势阱定态。密度有单位，概率无单位。This idealized instantaneous distribution illustrates integration; it is not a stationary infinite-well state. Density has units; probability is dimensionless.

ψ=Af,|A|=(∫|f|2dx)−1/2

L：区间长度；A：归一化因子；区间外 ψ = 0。L: interval length; A: normalization factor; ψ = 0 outside the interval.

先使总概率为 1，再计算区域概率。Set the total probability to 1 before finding regional probabilities.

例题与步骤Worked example+

已知 ψ = A sin(πx/L)，求 A 与左半区概率。Given ψ = A sin(πx/L), find A and the left-half probability.

  1. 利用 sin² 的积分确定振幅，取 A 为正实数。Use the integral of sin² to fix the amplitude, choosing A real and positive.

    1=|A|2∫0Lsin2πxLdx=|A|2L2 ⟹ A=2L

  2. 由对称性或直接积分，左右两半概率相等。By symmetry or direct integration, both halves have equal probability.

    P(0<x<L/2)=12,⟨x⟩=L2

整体相位不改变物理状态；已归一化后只能再乘模为 1 的常数并保持归一化。A global phase does not change the state; after normalization, only a factor of modulus 1 preserves normalization.

态叠加与干涉项Superposition and interference

Ψ=c1ψ1+c2ψ2
|Ψ|2=|c1ψ1|2+|c2ψ2|2+2Re(c1*c2ψ1*ψ2)

c1、c2：复振幅；星号表示复共轭；正交归一基底中 |c1|² + |c2|² = 1。c1, c2: complex amplitudes; * denotes complex conjugation; in an orthonormal basis |c1|² + |c2|² = 1.

先叠加概率振幅，再取模平方；相对相位决定干涉。Add probability amplitudes before squaring; relative phase determines interference.

例题与步骤Worked example+

在同一位置，两条相干路径的振幅均为 a。At one position, two coherent paths each contribute amplitude a.

  1. 同相叠加时，该点的概率密度增强。For equal phases, the local density increases.

    |a+a|2=4|a|2

  2. 相差 π 时，该点完全相消。For a phase difference of π, they cancel locally.

    |a−a|2=0

4|a|² 是该点密度，不是总概率；相干叠加与非相干概率相加不同。4|a|² is a local density, not the total probability; coherent addition differs from adding incoherent probabilities.

定态与时间相位Stationary states and time phase

H^ψn=Enψn,Ψn(r,t)=ψn(r)e−iEnt/ℏ
|Ψn(r,t)|2=|ψn(r)|2

En：能量本征值；ψn：空间本征函数；要求哈密顿量不显含时间。En: energy eigenvalue; ψn: spatial eigenfunction; the Hamiltonian must be time independent.

分离时间变量；定态的概率密度不随时间改变。Separate the time variable; a stationary state’s probability density is time independent.

例题与步骤Worked example+

已知 En > 0 的一个能量本征态，经过时间 t = πℏ/En。For an energy eigenstate with En > 0, consider time t = πℏ/En.

  1. 时间因子等于 −1。The time factor equals −1.

    e−iEnt/ℏ=e−iπ=−1

  2. 整体符号改变，但概率密度相同。The global sign changes but the density is unchanged.

    Ψn=−ψn,|Ψn|2=|ψn|2

不同能量本征态的叠加一般不是定态；定态也不表示波函数本身完全不变。A superposition of different energies is generally not stationary; the wave function itself can still acquire a time phase.

狄拉克 δ 的筛选与缩放Dirac delta: sifting and scaling

∫−∞∞f(x)δ(x−a)dx=f(a)
δ(bx)=1|b|δ(x),b≠0

δ：分布而非普通函数；f 在抽样点连续；这里 x 为无量纲变量。δ is a distribution rather than an ordinary function; f is continuous at the sampled point; x is dimensionless here.

从积分中抽取某一点的函数值，缩放时保留绝对值因子。Extract a function value from an integral, retaining the absolute-value scaling factor.

例题与步骤Worked example+

计算 ∫x²δ(−2x + 4)dx，全实轴积分。Evaluate ∫x²δ(−2x + 4)dx over the real line.

  1. 零点为 x = 2，导数大小为 2。The zero is x = 2 and the derivative magnitude is 2.

    δ(−2x+4)=12δ(x−2)

  2. 筛选 x² 在 2 处的值，再乘 1/2。Sample x² at 2, then multiply by 1/2.

    ∫x2δ(−2x+4)dx=12(22)=2

梯度、散度、旋度与拉普拉斯Gradient, divergence, curl and Laplacian

∇f=(∂xf,∂yf,∂zf),∇2f=∂x2f+∂y2f+∂z2f
∇·A=∂xAx+∂yAy+∂zAz,∇×(∇f)=0

f：标量场；A：矢量场；混合偏导可交换时使用旋度恒等式。f: scalar field; A: vector field; the curl identity assumes commuting mixed partial derivatives.

识别薛定谔方程中的空间微分算子。Identify the spatial differential operators used in the Schrödinger equation.

例题与步骤Worked example+

用无量纲坐标，令 f = x² + y² + z²。Using dimensionless coordinates, let f = x² + y² + z².

  1. 梯度由三个一阶偏导组成。The gradient consists of the three first partial derivatives.

    ∇f=(2x,2y,2z)

  2. 各二阶导数之和为 6；梯度的旋度为零。The second derivatives sum to 6; the curl of the gradient is zero.

    ∇2f=6,∇×∇f=0

格林恒等式与边界项Green identity and boundary terms

∫Ω(u∇2v−v∇2u)dV=∮∂Ω(u∂nv−v∂nu)dS

∂n：沿外法线方向的导数；∂Ω：区域边界；u、v 足够光滑。∂n: outward normal derivative; ∂Ω: region boundary; u and v are sufficiently smooth.

把体积分中的微分转移到另一个函数，同时保留边界项。Transfer derivatives between functions in a volume integral while keeping the boundary term.

例题与步骤Worked example+

一维区间上取 u = ψ*、v = φ，且两函数在端点为零。On a one-dimensional interval, take u = ψ* and v = φ, both zero at the endpoints.

  1. 一维格林恒等式右边是端点项。The one-dimensional Green identity gives an endpoint term.

    ∫0L(ψ*ϕ′′−ϕψ*′′)dx=[ψ*ϕ′−ϕψ*′]0L=0

  2. 因此在这些边界条件下，动能算符满足厄密积分关系。Thus the kinetic-energy operator satisfies the Hermitian integral relation under these boundary conditions.

    ⟨ψ|T^ϕ⟩=⟨T^ψ|ϕ⟩

无界微分算符的自伴性还涉及定义域；不能只看符号形式。Self-adjointness of an unbounded differential operator also depends on its domain, not just its formal expression.

位置、动量与动能算符Position, momentum and kinetic-energy operators

x^ψ=xψ,p^x=−iℏ∂x,T^=−ℏ22m∇2
H^=T^+V(r)

帽号表示算符；算符作用在其右边的波函数上。A hat denotes an operator, which acts on the wave function to its right.

用乘法和微分把可观测量表示为算符。Represent observables using multiplication and differentiation.

例题与步骤Worked example+

对 ψ(x) = A exp(ikx) 作用动量与动能算符。Apply momentum and kinetic-energy operators to ψ(x) = A exp(ikx).

  1. 一次求导后，波函数仅乘上常数 ℏk。After one derivative, the function is multiplied by the constant ℏk.

    p^xψ=ℏkψ

  2. 二次求导得到确定动能。Two derivatives give a definite kinetic energy.

    T^ψ=ℏ2k22mψ

对易子与算符次序Commutators and operator order

[A^,B^]=A^B^−B^A^,[x^,p^x]=iℏI^
[p^x,f(x)]=−iℏf′(x)
[A^,B^C^]=[A^,B^]C^+B^[A^,C^]

Î：单位算符；算符乘积中右边的算符先作用。Î: identity operator; in a product, the right-hand operator acts first.

判断交换算符次序是否改变结果。Determine whether reversing operator order changes the result.

例题与步骤Worked example+

求 [p̂x, x²]。Find [p̂x, x²].

  1. 取 f(x) = x²，其导数为 2x。Take f(x) = x², whose derivative is 2x.

    [p^x,x2]=−2iℏx

  2. 对任意合适的 ψ，结果都是 −2iℏxψ。For any suitable ψ, the result is −2iℏxψ.

    [p^x,x2]ψ=−2iℏxψ

内积、正交与展开系数Inner products, orthogonality and coefficients

⟨ϕ|ψ⟩=∫ϕ*ψdτ,⟨ϕm|ϕn⟩=δmn
ψ=∑ncnϕn,cn=⟨ϕn|ψ⟩

δmn：克罗内克符号；φn：正交归一基函数；cn：展开振幅。δmn: Kronecker delta; φn: orthonormal basis functions; cn: expansion amplitudes.

用内积提取某个基态分量，而不是直接把系数当概率。Use an inner product to extract a basis-state amplitude; coefficients themselves are not probabilities.

例题与步骤Worked example+

ψ = (φ1 + 2φ2)/√5，φ1、φ2 正交归一。ψ = (φ1 + 2φ2)/√5, with φ1 and φ2 orthonormal.

  1. 分别投影到两个基函数。Project onto each basis function.

    c1=15,c2=25

  2. 模平方之和为 1。Their squared moduli sum to 1.

    |c1|2+|c2|2=15+45=1

厄密算符与共轭次序Hermitian operators and adjoint order

A^†=A^,(A^B^)†=B^†A^†
⟨ψ|A^ϕ⟩=⟨A^ψ|ϕ⟩

†：厄密共轭（伴随）；公式应在合适的定义域和边界条件下使用。†: Hermitian adjoint; use these relations on an appropriate domain with appropriate boundary conditions.

检查可观测量的算符，并注意乘积取伴随时次序反转。Check an observable’s operator and reverse product order when taking the adjoint.

例题与步骤Worked example+

x̂ 与 p̂x 厄密时，x̂p̂x 是否也厄密？If x̂ and p̂x are Hermitian, is x̂p̂x Hermitian?

  1. 取伴随后次序反转；再用基本对易关系。Take the adjoint, reversing order, then use the canonical commutator.

    (x^p^x)†=p^xx^=x^p^x−iℏI^

  2. 通常不是；对称化的组合满足厄密关系。Generally no; the symmetrized combination satisfies the Hermitian relation.

    D^=12(x^p^x+p^xx^),D^†=D^

幺正算符Unitary operators

U^†U^=I^,U^−1=U^†
⟨Uϕ|Uψ⟩=⟨ϕ|ψ⟩

Û：幺正算符；它保持内积与归一化。Û: a unitary operator; it preserves inner products and normalization.

检查时间演化或表象变换是否保持总概率。Check that evolution or a representation change preserves total probability.

例题与步骤Worked example+

用整体相位算符 Û = exp(iθ)Î 作用于归一化态。Apply the global-phase operator Û = exp(iθ)Î to a normalized state.

  1. 伴随中的相位符号反转。The adjoint reverses the phase sign.

    U^†=e−iθI^,U^†U^=I^

  2. 波函数相位改变，但总概率与任意可观测量期望值不变。The phase changes, while total probability and observable expectation values remain unchanged.

    ‖Uψ‖2=‖ψ‖2=1

一般幺正演化可以改变物理状态；此例只是保持物理状态的整体相位变换。General unitary evolution can change the physical state; this example is a global phase, which leaves the state unchanged.

本征值与测量概率Eigenvalues and measurement probabilities

F^ϕn=fnϕn,ψ=∑ncnϕn,P(fn)=|cn|2
∑n|cn|2=1

fn：测量可能得到的本征值；φn：相应正交归一本征态。fn: a possible measurement eigenvalue; φn: its orthonormal eigenstate.

先在被测算符的本征基底展开，再对振幅取模平方。Expand in the measured operator’s eigenbasis, then square the amplitude modulus.

例题与步骤Worked example+

无限深势阱中，ψ = (ψ1 + 2ψ2)/√5。In an infinite well, ψ = (ψ1 + 2ψ2)/√5.

  1. 能量测量只能得到 E1 或 E2 = 4E1。Energy measurements can yield only E1 or E2 = 4E1.

    P(E1)=15,P(E2)=45

  2. 若重复准备此态并测量能量，长期频率趋于这些概率。For repeated preparations and measurements, the long-run frequencies approach these probabilities.

    ⟨E⟩=15E1+45(4E1)=175E1

简并本征值的概率要对对应正交本征态的概率求和；连续谱使用概率密度积分。For a degenerate eigenvalue, sum probabilities over its orthonormal eigenstates; continuous spectra require probability-density integrals.

期望值与标准差Expectation values and standard deviations

⟨F⟩=∫ψ*F^ψdτ=∑n|cn|2fn
(ΔF)2=⟨F2⟩−⟨F⟩2

ΔF：同一量子态中可观测量 F 的标准差；要求态已归一化。ΔF: standard deviation of observable F in the same quantum state; the state must be normalized.

区分一次测量结果、重复测量平均值和测量结果的分散程度。Distinguish a single outcome, the repeated-measurement mean and the spread of outcomes.

例题与步骤Worked example+

E1 和 4E1 分别以 1/5、4/5 的概率出现。E1 and 4E1 occur with probabilities 1/5 and 4/5.

  1. 计算一阶矩与二阶矩。Calculate the first and second moments.

    ⟨E⟩=175E1,⟨E2⟩=13E12

  2. 相减后开平方得到能量标准差。Subtract and take the square root to obtain the energy standard deviation.

    ΔE=13−(175)2E1=65E1

平均能量 3.4E1 不是本例可测得的单次能量值。The mean energy 3.4E1 is not a possible single-shot energy outcome in this example.

周期箱归一化Periodic-box normalization

ψn(x)=1Lei2πnx/L,pn=2πnℏL,n∈Z
ψ(x+L)=ψ(x),Δpspacing=2πℏL

L：周期长度；这里的 Δpspacing 指相邻动量值间距，并非标准差。L: period length; Δpspacing is the spacing of adjacent momentum values, not their standard deviation.

在有限周期区间归一化平面波，离散化动量取值。Normalize plane waves on a finite periodic interval and discretize momentum values.

例题与步骤Worked example+

周期长度 L = 10.0 nm，n = 1。Let the periodic length be L = 10.0 nm and n = 1.

  1. 使用周期性边界条件求动量。Use the periodic boundary condition to find the momentum.

    p1=hL≈6.63×10−26kgms−1

  2. 常数密度在一个周期内积分为 1。The constant density integrates to 1 over one period.

    ∫0L|ψ1|2dx=∫0LdxL=1

周期箱的边界条件不同于无限深势阱的硬壁条件；n 可以为负数或零。Periodic boundaries differ from infinite-well hard walls; n may be negative or zero.

轨道角动量与球谐函数Orbital angular momentum and spherical harmonics

L^=−iℏr×∇,L^z=−iℏ∂φ
L^2Ylm=l(l+1)ℏ2Ylm,L^zYlm=mℏYlm
l=0,1,…,m=−l,−l+1,…,l

l：轨道角量子数；m：磁量子数；Ylm：归一化球谐函数；此处 m 不是质量。l: orbital angular quantum number; m: magnetic quantum number; Ylm: normalized spherical harmonic; m here is not mass.

由量子数求 L² 与 Lz 的确定值。Obtain definite L² and Lz values from quantum numbers.

例题与步骤Worked example+

粒子角向态为 Y11。The angular state is Y11.

  1. l = 1，m = 1，代入两个本征方程。Substitute l = 1 and m = 1 into the two eigenvalue equations.

    L2=2ℏ2,Lz=ℏ

  2. 总角动量大小是 √2ℏ，不等于 z 分量。The total magnitude is √2ℏ, distinct from its z component.

    |L|=2ℏ

课件中的转子叠加例在求概率前需要归一化；本征值与平均值应分开计算。The rotor superposition example in the slides must be normalized before probabilities are calculated; distinguish eigenvalues from means.

位置–动量与一般不确定关系Position–momentum and general uncertainty

ΔAΔB≥12|⟨[A^,B^]⟩|
ΔxΔpx≥ℏ2

Δ：同一量子态中的标准差；尖括号表示该态中的期望值。Δ denotes standard deviation in the same quantum state; angle brackets denote expectation values in that state.

给定位置分布宽度，估算动量标准差的最低可能值。Given the position spread, estimate the smallest possible momentum standard deviation.

例题与步骤Worked example+

电子的位置标准差为 Δx = 0.100 nm。An electron has position standard deviation Δx = 0.100 nm.

  1. 先把位置标准差换算为米，再用不确定关系。Convert the position standard deviation to metres, then apply the inequality.

    Δpx≥1.055×10−342×10−10≈5.27×10−25kgms−1

  2. 在非相对论近似下，速度标准差至少约 5.79 × 10⁵ m/s。In the nonrelativistic approximation, the velocity standard deviation is at least about 5.79 × 10⁵ m/s.

    Δvx=Δpxme≳5.79×105ms−1

这是量子态自身的统计宽度下界，不是单纯由仪器误差造成；只有特定态达到等号。This is a bound on intrinsic state spreads, not merely instrumental error; equality holds only for particular states.

角动量对易与不确定度Angular momentum commutators and uncertainty

[L^x,L^y]=iℏL^z,[L^2,L^z]=0
ΔLxΔLy≥ℏ2|⟨Lz⟩|
(ΔLx)2=(ΔLy)2=ℏ22[l(l+1)−m2]in Ylm

ΔLx、ΔLy：角动量分量标准差；Ylm 中 ⟨Lx⟩ = ⟨Ly⟩ = 0。ΔLx, ΔLy: component standard deviations; in Ylm, ⟨Lx⟩ = ⟨Ly⟩ = 0.

说明 L² 和 Lz 可以同时确定，而横向分量仍有分散。Show that L² and Lz can be definite while transverse components have a spread.

例题与步骤Worked example+

在 Y11 态中计算横向角动量不确定度。Calculate transverse angular-momentum uncertainties in Y11.

  1. 代入 l = 1、m = 1 得到两个相同的标准差。Substitute l = 1 and m = 1 to obtain equal standard deviations.

    ΔLx=ΔLy=ℏ2

  2. 乘积恰好达到该态的下界，同时 ΔLz = 0。Their product saturates the bound in this state, while ΔLz = 0.

    ΔLxΔLy=ℏ22,ΔLz=0

谐振子的零点能Harmonic-oscillator zero-point energy

H^=p^22m+12mω2x^2
⟨H⟩≥ℏ28m(Δx)2+12mω2(Δx)2≥12ℏω

m：质量；ω：角频率；最低能量在均值为零的最小不确定高斯态达到。m: mass; ω: angular frequency; the minimum is attained by a zero-mean minimum-uncertainty Gaussian.

用不确定关系说明谐振子最低能量仍大于零。Use the uncertainty relation to show why the oscillator’s lowest energy is nonzero.

例题与步骤Worked example+

谐振子角频率为 ω = 1.00 × 10¹⁴ s⁻¹。An oscillator has angular frequency ω = 1.00 × 10¹⁴ s⁻¹.

  1. 对位置方差最小化，得到基态宽度。Minimize with respect to position variance to obtain the ground-state width.

    (Δx)2=ℏ2mω

  2. 代入最低能量公式。Substitute into the minimum-energy formula.

    E0=12ℏω≈5.27×10−21J≈0.0329eV

这里使用角频率 ω；若给普通频率 ν，应使用 E0 = hν/2。The input here is angular frequency ω; for ordinary frequency ν use E0 = hν/2.
