填空 1Fill-in 15 分points

真空中波长为 $\lambda$ 的光子，其能量 $E=$（　　　），动量大小 $p=$（　　　）。

A photon in vacuum has wavelength $\lambda$. Its energy is $E=$ (　　　), and momentum magnitude is $p=$ (　　　).

提示Hint

联用 $E=h\nu$、$c=\lambda\nu$ 和 $E=cp$。

Combine $E=h\nu$, $c=\lambda\nu$ and $E=cp$.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$hc/\lambda$；$h/\lambda$。

解析

  1. 真空中 $\nu=c/\lambda$，因此 $E=h\nu=hc/\lambda$。
  2. 光子静质量为零，$E=cp$，故 $p=E/c=h/\lambda=\hbar k$。

易错点：光子不能用非相对论的 $p=mv$ 并令静质量为零来计算动量。

Exam answer

$hc/\lambda$; $h/\lambda$.

Working

  1. In vacuum $\nu=c/\lambda$, so $E=hc/\lambda$.
  2. For a photon $E=cp$, hence $p=h/\lambda=\hbar k$.

Common error：Do not set photon momentum to zero by inserting its zero rest mass into the nonrelativistic formula $mv$.

填空 2Fill-in 25 分points

电子从静止经电压 $U>0$ 加速，忽略相对论效应，其德布罗意波长 $\lambda=$（　　　）。若电压变为 $4U$，波长变为原来的（　　　）倍。

An electron starts from rest and is accelerated through $U>0$. Neglect relativity. Its wavelength is $\lambda=$ (　　　). At $4U$, the wavelength is (　　　) times its original value.

提示Hint

先由电势能差求动能，再求动量。

Find kinetic energy from voltage, then momentum.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$h/\sqrt{2m_e eU}$；$1/2$。

解析

  1. 用电荷量大小 $e>0$，$eU=p^2/(2m_e)$，所以 $p=\sqrt{2m_e eU}$。
  2. $\lambda=h/p\propto U^{-1/2}$；电压乘4使动量乘2、波长除2。

易错点：该式取非相对论近似和从静止出发；不要把电压与能量的单位直接混用。

Exam answer

$h/\sqrt{2m_e eU}$; $1/2$.

Working

  1. With charge magnitude $e>0$, $eU=p^2/(2m_e)$, giving $p=\sqrt{2m_e eU}$.
  2. $\lambda=h/p\propto U^{-1/2}$, so quadrupling voltage halves wavelength.

Common error：This assumes nonrelativistic motion starting from rest. Keep voltage and energy units distinct.

填空 3Fill-in 35 分points

归一化波函数 $\Psi(\mathbf r,t)$ 对应的几率密度 $\rho=$（　　　）；全空间归一化条件为（　　　）。

For a normalized wave function $\Psi(\mathbf r,t)$, the probability density is $\rho=$ (　　　), and the whole-space normalization condition is (　　　).

提示Hint

密度与概率的区别在于是否已对体积积分。

A density becomes probability after integration over volume.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$\rho=|\Psi|^2$；$\int_{\mathbb R^3}|\Psi|^2dV=1$。

解析

  1. 体积元 $dV$ 中的概率为 $dP=|\Psi|^2dV$，而不是单独的 $|\Psi|^2$。
  2. 对全空间积分得到找到粒子的总概率，归一化后等于1。

易错点：波函数本身是概率幅；不能用 $\int\Psi dV=1$ 替代归一化。

Exam answer

$\rho=|\Psi|^2$; $\int_{\mathbb R^3}|\Psi|^2dV=1$.

Working

  1. The probability in $dV$ is $|\Psi|^2dV$; $|\Psi|^2$ alone is a density.
  2. Whole-space integration gives total probability one.

Common error：Normalize the squared modulus, not the amplitude integral.

填空 4Fill-in 45 分points

若 $\hat H\psi=E\psi$ 且势能不显含时间，相应含时定态可写为（　　　），其几率密度是否随时间变化？（　　　）

If $\hat H\psi=E\psi$ and the potential is time-independent, the corresponding time-dependent stationary state is (　　　). Does its probability density vary with time? (　　　)

提示Hint

时间因子是模长为1的相位。

The time factor is a phase of unit modulus.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$\Psi(\mathbf r,t)=\psi(\mathbf r)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$；不随时间变化。

解析

  1. 分离变量后时间函数满足 $i\hbar T\prime=ET$，所以 $T=e^{-iEt/\hbar}$。
  2. $|\Psi|^2=|\psi|^2|e^{-iEt/\hbar}|^2=|\psi|^2$。

易错点：定态不是波函数完全不含时间；不随时间变化的是其概率密度等定态统计性质。

Exam answer

$\Psi(\mathbf r,t)=\psi(\mathbf r)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$; no.

Working

  1. Separation gives $i\hbar T\prime=ET$ and $T=e^{-iEt/\hbar}$.
  2. The phase has unit modulus, so $|\Psi|^2=|\psi|^2$.

Common error：Stationary does not mean that the wave function has no time factor.

填空 5Fill-in 55 分points

以 $\rho$ 表示几率密度、$\mathbf j$ 表示几率流密度，局域几率守恒方程为（　　　）。该结论由波函数的（　　　）和薛定谔方程得到。

Using density $\rho$ and current $\mathbf j$, local probability conservation is (　　　). It follows from the wave function’s (　　　) together with the Schrödinger equation.

提示Hint

散度描述单位体积的净流出。

Divergence measures net outward flux per unit volume.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0$；统计解释。

解析

  1. 体积内概率的变化等于流入减去流出；写成微分形式就是连续性方程。
  2. 统计解释令 $\rho=|\Psi|^2$；实势能薛定谔方程与其共轭式给出守恒式，因此无需另立一个独立概率守恒假设。

易错点：局域守恒不要求 $\partial_t\rho=0$，空间流动可以造成局部密度变化。

Exam answer

$\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0$; statistical interpretation.

Working

  1. Probability change in a volume is inflow minus outflow, yielding the continuity equation.
  2. $\rho=|\Psi|^2$ comes from the statistical interpretation; the Schrödinger equation for a real potential gives the conservation law.

Common error：Local conservation does not require stationary density.

填空 6Fill-in 65 分points

$[\hat p_x,\hat x]=$（　　　）；$[\hat x,\hat p_y]=$（　　　）。

$[\hat p_x,\hat x]=$ (　　　); $[\hat x,\hat p_y]=$ (　　　).

提示Hint

交换两个算符的次序会改变对易括号的符号；不同方向另作判断。

Reversing a commutator changes its sign; different Cartesian directions commute.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$-i\hbar$；$0$。

解析

  1. 由 $[x,p_x]=i\hbar$ 和反对称性得 $[p_x,x]=-i\hbar$。
  2. $\partial_y x=0$，因此 $x(-i\hbar\partial_y f)-(-i\hbar\partial_y)(xf)=0$。

易错点：正则关系中的 $\delta_{ij}$ 不能省略后误用到不同方向。

Exam answer

$-i\hbar$; $0$.

Working

  1. Antisymmetry reverses $[x,p_x]=i\hbar$.
  2. Since $\partial_y x=0$, the product rule gives $[x,p_y]f=0$.

Common error：The Cartesian Kronecker delta matters.

填空 7Fill-in 75 分points

量子力学中力学量用（　　　）算符表示，以符合态叠加原理；还要求它为（　　　）算符，以保证本征值和平均值为实数。

An observable operator is (　　　) to respect superposition and (　　　) so that its eigenvalues and expectations are real.

提示Hint

一个条件涉及加法与数乘，另一个涉及伴随。

One condition concerns linear combinations; the other concerns the adjoint.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

线性；厄米。

解析

  1. 线性为 $F(c_1\psi_1+c_2\psi_2)=c_1F\psi_1+c_2F\psi_2$。
  2. 厄米为 $F^\dagger=F$（采用适当定义域）。它使可观测量的谱和期望值为实数。

易错点：“厄米”与“幺正”不是同一个定义。

Exam answer

Linear; Hermitian.

Working

  1. Linearity is $F(c_1\psi_1+c_2\psi_2)=c_1F\psi_1+c_2F\psi_2$.
  2. Hermiticity is $F^\dagger=F$ on a suitable domain and gives real observable eigenvalues and means.

Common error：Hermitian and unitary are different conditions.

填空 8Fill-in 85 分points

$u_1,u_2$ 是某力学量算符不同本征值对应的正交归一本征函数。体系处于 $\psi=(\sqrt3/2)u_1+(1/2)u_2$，测得对应第一个本征值的概率为（　　　），第二个为（　　　）。

Orthonormal $u_1,u_2$ have distinct eigenvalues of an observable. In $\psi=(\sqrt3/2)u_1+(1/2)u_2$, the probabilities of the first and second eigenvalues are (　　　) and (　　　).

提示Hint

先确认归一化，再取系数的模平方。

Check normalization, then square coefficient magnitudes.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$3/4$；$1/4$。

解析

  1. 范数为 $|\sqrt3/2|^2+|1/2|^2=1$，无需再归一化。
  2. 本征值互异且基函数正交归一，所以测量概率就是相应系数的模平方。

Exam answer

$3/4$; $1/4$.

Working

  1. The squared coefficients sum to one, so the state is normalized.
  2. For distinct eigenvalues in an orthonormal basis, probabilities are squared coefficient magnitudes.

Common error：The amplitude $\sqrt3/2$ is not itself a probability.

黑体温度由 $T$ 变为 $2T$，能量密度按波长分布的峰值波长变为原来的（　　）

A. 2倍
B. 4倍
C. 1/2倍
D. 不变

A black body is heated from $T$ to $2T$. Its peak wavelength becomes:

A. Twice as large
B. Four times as large
C. One half as large
D. Unchanged

提示Hint

使用峰值波长与温度的乘积为常量。

Use the constant product of peak wavelength and temperature.

C。

解析

Exam answer

C.

Working

  1. $\lambda_mT=b$ gives $\lambda_m(2T)=\lambda_m(T)/2$.
  2. The wavelength peak shifts shorter. Do not confuse the peak location with a power law for total emitted radiation.

For a fixed metal surface and fixed incident frequency above threshold, increasing intensity gives:

A. Higher threshold frequency

C. Larger maximum kinetic energy
D. Larger work function

单个光子的能量与单位时间的光子数分别由什么控制？

  2. More intense light supplies more photons per unit time, increasing emission rate and saturation current in the usual model.

关于 $\Psi(x,t)=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$，正确的是（　　）

For $\Psi(x,t)=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$, which is correct?

B. Position must be definite.
C. Every stationary state has zero probability current.

D.

  1. This is an energy eigenstate with eigenvalue $E$ and a time phase of unit modulus.
  2. Position can remain distributed. A real bound stationary state can have zero current, but a traveling plane wave has nonzero constant current, disproving C.

简答 1Short answer 15 分points

$\Psi(x,t)=Ae^{i(kx-\omega t)}$ 描述怎样的粒子状态？写出它的能量、动量与 $\omega,k$ 的关系，并说明为什么在全直线上不能普通归一化为1。

检查模方是否随位置衰减。

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

它描述能量和动量确定的自由粒子平面波，$E=\hbar\omega$、$p=\hbar k$。当 $A\ne0$ 时 $|\Psi|^2=|A|^2$ 在全直线为常数，积分发散，因此不能普通归一化为1。

解析

  1. 分别作用 $i\hbar\partial_t$ 和 $-i\hbar\partial_x$，得到 $E\Psi$ 和 $p\Psi$。
  2. $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|A|^2dx=\infty$。理想平面波可用δ广义归一化，或在有限周期箱中作箱归一化；实际局域化态用波包描述。

易错点：不能通过把 $A$ 随意设得“很小但非零”使无穷区间的常数积分变成1。

Exam answer

It is a free-particle plane wave with definite $E=\hbar\omega$ and $p=\hbar k$. Its nonzero constant density has a divergent whole-line integral, so ordinary normalization to one is impossible.

Working

  1. Acting with $i\hbar\partial_t$ and $-i\hbar\partial_x$ gives the energy and momentum eigenvalues.
  2. Use delta normalization for ideal waves, a finite periodic box for box normalization, or a wave packet for a localized physical state.

Common error：Making a nonzero constant amplitude small does not make its whole-line norm finite.

证明 $[\hat p_x,x^2]=-2i\hbar x$。要求写出算符对任意适当函数的作用过程。

Prove $[\hat p_x,x^2]=-2i\hbar x$ by applying the operators to an arbitrary suitable function.

提示Hint

乘积求导有两个来自 $x$ 的项。

The product rule supplies the derivative of $x^2$ as well as that of the test function.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$[\hat p_x,x^2]=-2i\hbar x$。

解析

  1. 对任意 $f$，$\hat p_x(x^2f)=-i\hbar(2xf+x^2f\prime)$。
  2. $x^2\hat p_xf=-i\hbar x^2f\prime$。
  3. 相减得 $[\hat p_x,x^2]f=-2i\hbar xf$，因 $f$ 任意即得算符等式。

易错点：这是原卷基本对易证明的变式，关键仍是乘积法则，不能把 $x^2$ 当常数。

Exam answer

$[\hat p_x,x^2]=-2i\hbar x$.

Working

  1. $p_x(x^2f)=-i\hbar(2xf+x^2f\prime)$ for arbitrary suitable $f$.
  2. $x^2p_xf=-i\hbar x^2f\prime$.
  3. Subtract to get $[p_x,x^2]f=-2i\hbar xf$ and hence the operator identity.

Common error：The product rule is essential; $x^2$ is not constant under differentiation.

简答 3Short answer 35 分points

将归一化波函数整体乘以 $i$ 是否改变所描述的量子态？若只给叠加态的一个分量乘以 $i$，是否也一定不改变物理预言？说明理由。

Does multiplying a normalized wave function by $i$ change its physical state? If only one component of a superposition is multiplied by $i$, are all predictions necessarily unchanged? Explain.

提示Hint

比较共同因子能否从整个波函数提出。

Check whether the phase is a common factor of the entire state.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

整体乘 $i$ 只改变整体相位，物理态和测量概率不变；只改变一个分量通常改变相对相位，物理预言不一定相同。

解析

  1. 整体相位 $e^{i\alpha}$ 的模长为1：$|e^{i\alpha}\psi|^2=|\psi|^2$，期望值中的相位也相消。
  2. 对 $c_1\psi_1+c_2\psi_2$，仅把第二项改为 $ic_2\psi_2$，会改变交叉项 $2\operatorname{Re}(c_1^*c_2\psi_1^*\psi_2)$，从而可改变干涉图样。
  3. 在原本征基测量的权重 $|c_n|^2$ 可能不变，但不能据此断言所有其他测量也不变。

易错点：“相位不影响概率”只能无条件用于整体相位，不能用于任意相对相位变化。

Exam answer

A global factor $i$ changes only overall phase and leaves the physical state unchanged. Applying it to one component generally changes relative phase and can change predictions.

Working

  1. A unit-modulus global phase cancels in $|\psi|^2$ and in expectation values.
  2. Changing one component alters the interference cross term $2\operatorname{Re}(c_1^*c_2\psi_1^*\psi_2)$ and can change spatial interference.
  3. Weights in the original eigenbasis may remain unchanged, without making every other measurement unchanged.

Common error：Only global phase is universally unobservable; relative phase can matter.

简答 4Short answer 45 分points

“两个厄米算符的乘积仍是厄米算符”，这句话是否总成立？在本课程通常假设下给出成立条件和证明。

Is a product of two Hermitian operators always Hermitian? State and prove the usual course-level condition.

提示Hint

取乘积的伴随时要反转次序。

Taking an adjoint reverses the order of factors.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

不总成立。在乘积及伴随都定义良好的共同定义域上，$AB$ 为厄米的条件是 $AB=BA$。

解析

  1. 由 $A^\dagger=A$、$B^\dagger=B$，得 $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger=BA$。
  2. 要等于原来的 $AB$，须有 $BA=AB$，即对易。
  3. 例如 $xp_x$ 不厄米，因为其伴随 $p_xx=xp_x-i\hbar$；对称化 $(xp_x+p_xx)/2$ 在适当域下是厄米形式。

易错点：对无界微分算符还需兼容定义域，不能仅凭形式代数断言所有边界下都自伴。

Exam answer

No. On compatible domains where these products and adjoints are well-defined, $AB$ is Hermitian when $AB=BA$.

Working

  1. For Hermitian factors, $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger=BA$.
  2. Equality with $AB$ therefore requires commutation.
  3. For example, $(xp_x)^\dagger=p_xx=xp_x-i\hbar$; a symmetrized product $(xp_x+p_xx)/2$ has Hermitian form on a suitable domain.

Common error：Unbounded differential operators also require domain compatibility; formal algebra alone does not settle every boundary-value problem.

计算 1Calculation 120 分points

电子处于一维无限深方势阱：$0<x<a$ 时 $V=0$，阱外 $V=\infty$，$a>0$。

（1）求能量本征值及归一化空间波函数。（12分）

（2）电子处于第一激发态 $n=2$，求 $\langle x\rangle$ 及在区间 $[a/4,3a/4]$ 内出现的概率。（8分）

An electron is in an infinite well with $V=0$ for $0<x<a$ and $V=\infty$ outside, $a>0$.

(1) Find the energy eigenvalues and normalized spatial eigenfunctions. (12 points)

(2) In the first excited state $n=2$, find $\langle x\rangle$ and the probability in $[a/4,3a/4]$. (8 points)

提示Hint

第（1）问用两端零边界；第（2）问先利用密度对称性，再积分，不能把区间概率当成区间长度比例。

Use zero endpoint conditions. Then exploit reflection symmetry and integrate the density, rather than assuming uniform probability.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$E_n=n^2\pi^2\hbar^2/(2m_ea^2)$，$\psi_n=\sqrt{2/a}\sin(n\pi x/a)$（阱内；阱外为0），$n=1,2,\ldots$。在 $n=2$ 态，$\langle x\rangle=a/2$，$P(a/4\le x\le3a/4)=1/2$。

解析

  1. 阱内 $-\hbar^2\psi\prime\prime/(2m_e)=E\psi$；通解 $A\sin kx+B\cos kx$。由 $\psi(0)=\psi(a)=0$ 得 $B=0$、$ka=n\pi$，排除零函数 $n=0$，得 $E_n$。
  2. 由 $|A|^2\int_0^a\sin^2(n\pi x/a)dx=|A|^2a/2=1$ 得 $A=\sqrt{2/a}$。
  3. 对 $n=2$，$\rho_2=(2/a)\sin^2(2\pi x/a)$ 关于 $a/2$ 对称，所以 $\langle x\rangle=a/2$；也可直接代入 $\int_0^a x\rho_2dx$。
  4. 区间概率 $P=(2/a)\int_{a/4}^{3a/4}\sin^2(2\pi x/a)dx=[x/a-\sin(4\pi x/a)/(4\pi)]_{a/4}^{3a/4}=1/2$。
  5. 这里两个端点正弦项恰为零；得到1/2是积分的结果，不是所有半阱宽区间都具有1/2概率。

易错点：第一激发态是 $n=2$，不是 $n=1$；位置均值在中心并不要求中心概率密度最大。

参考分配：方程与边界4、能级4、归一化波函数4；位置均值及理由4、区间概率积分及结果4。

Exam answer

$E_n=n^2\pi^2\hbar^2/(2m_ea^2)$ and $\psi_n=\sqrt{2/a}\sin(n\pi x/a)$ inside, zero outside, for $n=1,2,\ldots$. At $n=2$, $\langle x\rangle=a/2$ and $P[a/4,3a/4]=1/2$.

Working

  1. The interior equation is $-\hbar^2\psi\prime\prime/(2m_e)=E\psi$, with solution $A\sin kx+B\cos kx$. The zero endpoints give $B=0$ and $ka=n\pi$, excluding $n=0$, yielding the energy levels.
  2. Normalization gives $|A|^2a/2=1$ and a positive real $A=\sqrt{2/a}$.
  3. $\rho_2=(2/a)\sin^2(2\pi x/a)$ is symmetric about $a/2$, giving the mean $a/2$; direct integration agrees.
  4. $P=(2/a)\int_{a/4}^{3a/4}\sin^2(2\pi x/a)dx=[x/a-\sin(4\pi x/a)/(4\pi)]_{a/4}^{3a/4}=1/2$.
  5. The sine terms vanish at these particular endpoints; the result does not justify assuming uniform density.

Common error：The first excited state has $n=2$. A central mean does not require a density maximum at the center.
