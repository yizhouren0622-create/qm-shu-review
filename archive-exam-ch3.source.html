第 1 题Question 18 分points

写出 Dirac δ 函数的定义，并说明其"筛选性质"。计算下列积分：
$$\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(x)\,\delta(-2x+4)\,dx = \;?$$

Define the Dirac delta distribution through its integral action, explain the sifting property, and evaluate $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\delta(-2x+4)dx$.

提示Hint

先使用缩放性质，再使用筛选性质；δ 不是普通点值函数。

Scale the distribution first, then use sifting; delta is not an ordinary pointwise function.

参考答案与步骤Answer & working

1. δ 是分布，以对测试函数的积分作用定义，不把 $\delta(0)$ 当作普通数值。筛选性质：$\displaystyle\int f(x)\delta(x)\,dx=f(0)$，$\displaystyle\int f(x)\delta(x-a)\,dx=f(a)$。

由缩放性质 $\delta(ax)=\dfrac{1}{|a|}\delta(x)$，且 $\delta(-x)=\delta(x)$（偶对称）：

$\delta(-2x+4)=\delta\!\big(-2(x-2)\big)=\dfrac{1}{|-2|}\delta(x-2)=\dfrac12\delta(x-2)$

故 $\displaystyle\int f(x)\delta(-2x+4)\,dx=\int f(x)\cdot\tfrac12\delta(x-2)\,dx=\dfrac{f(2)}{2}$。

The delta is a distribution defined by $\int f(x)\delta(x-a)dx=f(a)$ for suitable test functions, not an ordinary function with a numerical value at zero. Since $\delta(-2(x-2))=\delta(x-2)/2$, the integral is $f(2)/2$.

Explain why observable operators are (a) linear, referring to superposition, and (b) Hermitian, referring to real measurement outcomes and expectation values.

提示Hint

Connect linearity with superposition and Hermiticity with real outcomes.

参考答案与步骤Answer & working

2.（a）线性算符满足 $\hat{F}(c_1\psi_1+c_2\psi_2)=c_1\hat{F}\psi_1+c_2\hat{F}\psi_2$。它源自态叠加原理：若 $\psi_1,\psi_2$ 是体系可能态，则 $c_1\psi_1+c_2\psi_2$ 也必是可能态；为使叠加态下力学量仍可由各分量线性叠加描述，算符必须线性。也正因如此，薛定谔方程才必须是线性方程。

(b) On a suitable domain, a Hermitian observable has real eigenvalues and real expectation values, as required of measured quantities. The operator-domain assumptions matter for differential operators.

第 3 题Question 314 分points

判断下列算符是否为厄米算符，并简要给出理由。
  1. $\hat{x}\hat{p}_x$（位置算符与动量算符之积）
  2. $\hat{H}=\dfrac{\hat{p}^{\,2}}{2m}+U(x)$（$U(x)$ 为实函数）
  3. $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}$
  4. $\,i\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}$

统一假设：采用使分部积分边界项消失的适当定义域与边界条件；不能只凭算符的形式忽略定义域。

Determine whether each operator is Hermitian and explain briefly: (1) $\hat x\hat p_x$; (2) $\hat H=\hat p^2/(2m)+U(x)$ for real $U$; (3) $\partial_x$; (4) $i\partial_x$. Use appropriate operator domains and boundary conditions that remove integration-by-parts boundary terms.

提示Hint

取伴随时乘积次序反转；导数项要检查边界。

Taking an adjoint reverses a product; derivative operators require boundary checks.

参考答案与步骤Answer & working

3.

（1）$\hat{x}\hat{p}_x$ 不是厄米。$\hat{x}$、$\hat{p}_x$ 均厄米，但二者不对易 $[\hat{x},\hat{p}_x]=i\hbar\ne0$。由 $(\hat{A}\hat{B})^+=\hat{B}^+\hat{A}^+=\hat{p}_x\hat{x}\ne\hat{x}\hat{p}_x$，故乘积非厄米。（两厄密算符之积仅当对易时才厄米。）

（2）$\hat{H}$ 是厄米。$\hat{p}$ 厄米且 $[\hat{p},\hat{p}]=0$，故 $\hat{p}^2$ 厄米；$U(x)$ 为实函数，乘以波函数的算符厄米；两厄密算符之和仍厄米。

（3）$\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}$ 不是厄米。由分部积分可得其转置 $\widetilde{\partial/\partial x}=-\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}$，于是其厄密共轭 $\left(\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)^+=\widetilde{\left(\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)}{}^*=-\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}\ne\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}$。

（4）在上述边界条件与定义域下，$i\partial_x=-\hat p_x/\hbar$，是厄米动量算符的实数倍，因此是厄米的。注意正确关系为 $\hat p_x/\hbar=-i\partial_x$。也可由分部积分验证 $\int\psi^*(i\phi\prime)dx=\int(i\psi\prime)^*\phi dx$，前提是边界项为零。

以上微分算符的厄米性均以合适的定义域和边界条件为前提。

  1. $xp_x$ is not Hermitian: $(xp_x)^\dagger=p_xx=xp_x-i\hbar$.
  2. $H$ is Hermitian for real $U$ and suitable domains/boundary conditions.
  3. $\partial_x$ is anti-Hermitian under boundary conditions removing the surface term: $(\partial_x)^\dagger=-\partial_x$.
  4. $i\partial_x=-p_x/\hbar$ is Hermitian under those same domain assumptions, since it is a real multiple of $p_x$. In particular, $p_x/\hbar=-i\partial_x$.

第 4 题Question 410 分points

已知基本对易关系 $[\hat{x},\hat{p}_x]=i\hbar$。试用"算符作用于任意波函数 $\psi$"的方法，证明：
$$[\hat{p}_x,\,f(\hat{x})] = -i\hbar\,\frac{df}{dx}$$

Starting with $[\hat x,\hat p_x]=i\hbar$, prove $[\hat p_x,f(\hat x)]=-i\hbar\,df/dx$ by applying the operators to an arbitrary smooth wave function.

提示Hint

先算 $\hat p_x(f\psi)$，使用乘积求导法则，再减去 $f\hat p_x\psi$。

Expand $\hat p_x(f\psi)$ by the product rule, then subtract $f\hat p_x\psi$.

参考答案与步骤Answer & working

4. 对任意波函数 $\psi$，分别计算两侧作用：

$\hat{p}_x\big[f(x)\psi\big]=\left(-i\hbar\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)(f\psi)=-i\hbar\big(f'\psi+f\psi'\big)$（莱布尼茨法则）

$f(x)\big[\hat{p}_x\psi\big]=f(x)\left(-i\hbar\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}\psi\right)=-i\hbar f\psi'$

两式相减：$\big[\hat{p}_x,f(x)\big]\psi=\hat{p}_x(f\psi)-f(\hat{p}_x\psi)=-i\hbar f'\psi=\left(-i\hbar\dfrac{df}{dx}\right)\psi$

因 $\psi$ 任意，故 $[\hat{p}_x,f(\hat{x})]=-i\hbar\dfrac{df}{dx}$。$\square$

  1. $p_x(f\psi)=-i\hbar(f'\psi+f\psi')$.
  2. $f(p_x\psi)=-i\hbar f\psi'$.
  3. Subtract: $[p_x,f]\psi=-i\hbar f'\psi$.

Because this holds for arbitrary suitable $\psi$, $[p_x,f(x)]=-i\hbar f'(x)$.

第 5 题Question 510 分points

证明动量算符 $\hat{p}_x=-i\hbar\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}$ 是厄米算符。
要求：写出关键的分部积分步骤，并指出证明中用到哪条边界条件。

Prove that $\hat p_x=-i\hbar\partial_x$ is Hermitian on the course’s chosen domain. Show the integration-by-parts step and state a boundary condition that makes its boundary term vanish.

提示Hint

分部积分后，检查 $[\psi^*\phi]$ 的端点差是否为零。

Integrate by parts and inspect the endpoint difference $[\psi^*\phi]$.

参考答案与步骤Answer & working

5. 对任意 $\psi,\phi$：

$\displaystyle\int\psi^*\,\hat{p}_x\phi\,dx=\int\psi^*\!\left(-i\hbar\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)\phi\,dx=-i\hbar\int\psi^*\phi'\,dx$

分部积分：$=-i\hbar\Big[\psi^*\phi\Big]_{-\infty}^{+\infty}+i\hbar\int(\psi^*)'\phi\,dx$

选取足够光滑、在无穷远衰减的函数作为定义域，并采用边界条件：$|x|\to\infty$ 时 $\psi,\phi\to0$，故边界项为零：

$=i\hbar\int(\psi^*)'\phi\,dx=\int\!\left(-i\hbar\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}\psi\right)^{\!*}\!\phi\,dx=\int(\hat{p}_x\psi)^*\phi\,dx$

即 $\hat{p}_x^{\,+}=\hat{p}_x$，故 $\hat{p}_x$ 厄米。关键边界条件：$\psi,\phi\to0\;(|x|\to\infty)$。$\square$

$$\int\psi^*p_x\phi\,dx=-i\hbar[\psi^*\phi]_a^b+i\hbar\int(\psi^*)'\phi\,dx.$$

If the boundary term vanishes, this equals $\int(p_x\psi)^*\phi dx$. Sufficient conditions in this course include suitable decay on the whole line or matching periodic endpoint values on a box. The domain must be specified; square integrability alone is not a universal pointwise boundary proof.

Momentum eigenstates:

  1. Write the one-dimensional momentum eigenvalue equation. Explain why an ideal de Broglie plane wave on the whole line cannot have norm one and how delta normalization is used instead. (6 points)
  2. Explain box normalization. Derive the discrete momenta from $\psi(x+L)=\psi(x)$. What happens to their spacing as $L\to\infty$? (9 points)

提示Hint

先求微分方程的平面波解；周期边界要求相位绕一圈增加 $2\pi n$。

Solve for a plane wave; periodicity requires a phase change of $2\pi n$.

参考答案与步骤Answer & working

7. （1）一维动量本征方程：$\hat{p}_x\psi_p(x)=p_x\psi_p(x)$，即 $-i\hbar\dfrac{d\psi_p}{dx}=p_x\psi_p$，解为平面波 $\psi_p(x)=C\,e^{ip_xx/\hbar}$。该解在 $|x|\to\infty$ 不趋于零而是等幅振荡，$\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\psi_p|^2 dx=|C|^2\!\int dx$ 发散，无法归一化为1。利用 $\displaystyle\int e^{i(p-p')x/\hbar}dx=2\pi\hbar\,\delta(p-p')$，取 $C=1/\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}$ 即可归一化为 δ 函数：$\langle p|p'\rangle=\delta(p-p')$。

（2）箱归一化：把粒子限制在边长 $L$ 的箱中，要求波函数在两对边对应点相等，即周期性边界条件 $\psi(x+L)=\psi(x)$。代入 $\psi_p=Ce^{ipx/\hbar}$ 得 $e^{ipL/\hbar}=1$，故 $p$ 只能取分立值：$p_n=\dfrac{2\pi\hbar}{L}\,n$（$n$ 为整数）。此时 $\psi$ 在箱内可正常归一化为1。相邻本征值间隔 $\Delta p=\dfrac{2\pi\hbar}{L}$，与 $L$ 成反比；当 $L\to\infty$ 时 $\Delta p\to0$，分立谱过渡回连续谱。周期性边界条件本质上也是动量算符厄米性所要求的边界条件。

  1. $-i\hbar\psi_p'=p\psi_p$ gives $\psi_p=Ce^{ipx/\hbar}$. Its constant modulus makes its whole-line norm divergent. Taking $C=(2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2}$ gives generalized normalization $\langle p|p'\rangle=\delta(p-p')$.
  2. In a periodic interval of length $L$, $e^{ipL/\hbar}=1$ gives $p_n=2\pi\hbar n/L$ for integer $n$. The normalized amplitude is $1/\sqrt L$.
  3. $\Delta p=2\pi\hbar/L\to0$ as $L\to\infty$, recovering a continuous spectrum. A periodic box is a normalization device, distinct from an infinite-wall well.

四、测不准关系 · 15 分IV. Uncertainty · 15 points

第 8 题Question 815 分points

利用坐标—动量测不准关系 $\Delta x\cdot\Delta p_x \ge \dfrac{\hbar}{2}$，推导线性谐振子的零点能 $E_0=\dfrac{1}{2}\hbar\omega$。

先给出能量下限，再说明为什么这个下限能够达到。

Use $\Delta x\Delta p_x\ge\hbar/2$ to derive a lower bound on the energy of an oscillator centered at $x=0$, and explain why the bound is attained, giving $E_0=\hbar\omega/2$.

提示Hint

中心谐振子本征态的宇称给出 $\langle x\rangle=0$；实且衰减的波函数给出 $\langle p\rangle=-i\hbar[\psi^2]/2=0$。用 $y=(\Delta x)^2$ 求能量下限，再说明高斯基态能取等号。

Parity gives $\langle x\rangle=0$ for a centered oscillator eigenstate; a real decaying wave function gives $\langle p\rangle=-i\hbar[\psi^2]/2=0$. Minimize the bound using $y=(\Delta x)^2$ and explain its Gaussian saturation.

参考答案与步骤Answer & working

中心位于 $x=0$ 的谐振子本征态有确定宇称，$|\psi_n|^2$ 为偶函数，因此 $\langle x\rangle=0$。可取实且在无穷远衰减的波函数，于是 $\langle p\rangle=\int\psi_n(-i\hbar\partial_x)\psi_n dx=-i\hbar[\psi_n^2]_{-\infty}^{\infty}/2=0$。实波函数本身不保证位置平均值为零。故 $\langle x^2\rangle=(\Delta x)^2$，$\langle p^2\rangle=(\Delta p)^2$。

$\displaystyle E=\overline{H}=\frac{\overline{p^2}}{2m}+\frac12 m\omega^2\overline{x^2}=\frac{(\Delta p)^2}{2m}+\frac12 m\omega^2(\Delta x)^2$

由测不准关系 $(\Delta x)^2(\Delta p)^2\ge\dfrac{\hbar^2}{4}$。均方偏差不能同时为零，$E$ 不能为零；先由不等式得到下限；若能取等号，则：$(\Delta p)^2=\dfrac{\hbar^2}{4(\Delta x)^2}$，代入：

$\displaystyle E\ge\frac{\hbar^2}{8m(\Delta x)^2}+\frac12 m\omega^2(\Delta x)^2$，令 $y=(\Delta x)^2>0$，则 $E(y)=\dfrac{\hbar^2}{8my}+\frac12 m\omega^2 y$。

求极值：$\dfrac{dE}{dy}=-\dfrac{\hbar^2}{8my^2}+\frac12 m\omega^2=0\;\Rightarrow\;y=\dfrac{\hbar}{2m\omega}$。

代回：$E_{\min}=\dfrac{\hbar^2}{8m\cdot\hbar/(2m\omega)}+\frac12 m\omega^2\cdot\dfrac{\hbar}{2m\omega}=\dfrac{\hbar\omega}{4}+\dfrac{\hbar\omega}{4}$

故线性谐振子零点能 $\boxed{E_0=\dfrac12\hbar\omega}$。它正是测不准关系所强制要求的最小能量。$\square$

可达性：归一化高斯态 $\psi_0=(m\omega/(\pi\hbar))^{1/4}e^{-m\omega x^2/(2\hbar)}$ 满足 $(\Delta x)^2=\hbar/(2m\omega)$，并使不确定关系取等号，因此下限确实是基态能量。

  1. For centered oscillator eigenstates, parity makes $x|\psi_n|^2$ odd, so $\langle x\rangle=0$. For a real, decaying $\psi_n$, $\langle p\rangle=-i\hbar\int\psi_n\psi_n'dx=-i\hbar[\psi_n^2]/2=0$. Reality alone does not imply zero position mean.
  2. Set $y=(\Delta x)^2>0$. The uncertainty relation gives $\langle H\rangle\ge\hbar^2/(8my)+m\omega^2y/2$.
  3. The derivative vanishes at $y=\hbar/(2m\omega)$, yielding the lower bound $\hbar\omega/2$.
  4. The normalized Gaussian $\psi_0=(m\omega/(\pi\hbar))^{1/4}e^{-m\omega x^2/(2\hbar)}$ has that variance and saturates $\Delta x\Delta p=\hbar/2$. Thus the bound is attained: $E_0=\hbar\omega/2$.
