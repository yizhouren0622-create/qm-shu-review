原卷 一、1 · PDF 1Source paper I.1 · PDF 1

Compton 效应证实了光具有（　　　）、Davisson 和 Germer 的实验证实了电子具有（　　　）。

The Compton effect confirms the (　　　) of light; the Davisson–Germer experiment confirms the (　　　) of electrons.

提示Hint

区分“光子碰撞中交换能量和动量”与“晶体衍射”。

Distinguish photon collision kinematics from diffraction by a crystal.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

粒子性；波动性。

解析

  1. 康普顿效应可按光子与电子碰撞处理，光子携带 $E=h\nu$、$p=h/\lambda$；这体现光的粒子性。
  2. Davisson–Germer 实验中电子束经晶体散射形成衍射极大，验证电子的波动性及 $\lambda=h/p$。

易错点：不要把两个实验的证据方向颠倒。实验分别突出某一方面，不表示光或电子只具有这一种性质。

Exam answer

Particle character; wave character.

Working

  1. Compton scattering is described using photon energy $E=h\nu$ and momentum $p=h/\lambda$ exchanged with an electron, demonstrating the particle aspect of light.
  2. Electron diffraction from a crystal in Davisson–Germer verifies the wave aspect and $\lambda=h/p$.

Common error：The two experiments emphasize different aspects; neither says the object has only that aspect.

原卷 一、2 · PDF 1Source paper I.2 · PDF 1

波函数应满足的标准条件是（　　　）、（　　　）、（　　　）。

The standard regularity conditions for a wave function are (　　　), (　　　), and (　　　).

提示Hint

按课件的三个性质回答，不是写概率积分。

Give the three regularity properties in the slides, rather than a probability integral.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

连续性、有限性、单值性（顺序可调）。

解析

  1. 课件把这三个性质称为波函数的“标准化条件”：同一位置的波函数取值应唯一，在通常讨论的区域内有限且连续。
  2. “归一化”另指平方可积态满足 $\int |\Psi|^2\,dV=1$，不能替代上面的三个空。

易错点：不要把“标准条件”和“归一化条件”混成同一问。涉及奇异势能时，还需按具体模型讨论匹配条件。

Exam answer

Continuous, finite, and single-valued (any order).

Working

  1. These are the three regularity conditions named in the slides. A point has a unique wave-function value, finite and continuous in the usual domain considered.
  2. Normalization of a square-integrable state is a separate condition, $\int |\Psi|^2dV=1$.

Common error：Do not replace one regularity condition with normalization. Singular potentials require model-specific matching conditions.

原卷 一、3 · PDF 1Source paper I.3 · PDF 1

对易式 $[\hat F,\hat G]$ 等于（　　　）。

The commutator $[\hat F,\hat G]$ equals (　　　).

提示Hint

写出两种作用次序的差，注意先后顺序。

Subtract the products in opposite orders, retaining their order.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$[\hat F,\hat G]=\hat F\hat G-\hat G\hat F$。

解析

  1. $\hat F\hat G\psi$ 的意思是先让 $\hat G$ 作用，再让 $\hat F$ 作用。
  2. 若对定义域内的函数两种次序作用相同，即 $[\hat F,\hat G]=0$，称为对易；否则称为不对易。

易错点：$[\hat G,\hat F]=-[\hat F,\hat G]$，所以反写次序会改变符号。

Exam answer

$[\hat F,\hat G]=\hat F\hat G-\hat G\hat F$.

Working

  1. In $\hat F\hat G\psi$, $\hat G$ acts first. The commutator compares this with the reverse order.
  2. If the difference vanishes on the appropriate domain, the operators commute.

Common error：Reversing the labels reverses the sign: $[\hat G,\hat F]=-[\hat F,\hat G]$.

原卷 一、4 · PDF 1Source paper I.4 · PDF 1

已知一粒子的能量是 $E$，根据德布罗意关系，它波动形式对应的频率应该是（　　　）。

A particle has energy $E$. By the de Broglie relation, its corresponding wave frequency is (　　　).

提示Hint

区分普通频率与角频率。

Distinguish ordinary frequency from angular frequency.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$\nu=E/h$。

解析

  1. 由 $E=h\nu=\hbar\omega$ 得普通频率 $\nu=E/h$，角频率 $\omega=E/\hbar$。
  2. 两者满足 $\omega=2\pi\nu$，对应平面波 $e^{-iEt/\hbar}$ 的相位变化。

易错点：题目问“频率”，通常填 $E/h$；$E/\hbar$ 是角频率。应沿用题目和模型给定的能量零点约定。

Exam answer

$\nu=E/h$.

Working

  1. $E=h\nu=\hbar\omega$ gives ordinary frequency $\nu=E/h$ and angular frequency $\omega=E/\hbar$.
  2. They obey $\omega=2\pi\nu$ and generate the time phase $e^{-iEt/\hbar}$.

Common error：$E/\hbar$ is angular frequency, not ordinary frequency. Use the energy convention specified by the model.

填空 5Fill-in 55 分points

原卷 一、5 · PDF 1Source paper I.5 · PDF 1

几率守恒具有局域性质，当空间某处几率减少了，必然另外一些地方几率增加，使总几率（　　　），并伴随着某种流来实现这种变化。几率守恒定律是（　　　）和（　　　）的推论，并不是量子力学中的一条独立假设。由几率密度和几率流密度的表达式中可知，波函数在空间坐标的变化全部区域内应是（　　　）。

Probability conservation is local: a decrease in one region is balanced elsewhere, keeping total probability (　　　), with a current realizing the change. It follows from (　　　) and (　　　), rather than being an independent postulate. From the density and current expressions, the wave function throughout the spatial domain should be (　　　).

提示Hint

最后一空应把课件第51页的整项要求写全，而不只写三个标准条件中的一个词。

For the last blank, include the full requirement on slide PDF p. 51, rather than just one of the three regularity words.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

不变；波函数的统计解释；薛定谔方程；连续的，且有连续的微商。

解析

  1. 按统计解释，归一化态的密度 $\rho=\Psi^*\Psi$。对实势能的薛定谔方程及其复共轭式作差，可得 $\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0$，其中 $\mathbf j=\frac{\hbar}{2mi}(\Psi^*\nabla\Psi-\Psi\nabla\Psi^*)$。
  2. 对区域 $\Omega$ 积分：$\frac{d}{dt}\int_\Omega\rho\,dV=-\oint_{\partial\Omega}\mathbf j\cdot d\mathbf S$。区域内概率的减少对应向外的概率流。
  3. 在全空间无净流出、或适当封闭边界条件下，总概率不随时间变化；已归一化时恒为1。
  4. 课件第51页把最后一项写作“连续的，且有连续的微商”，本题按这项完整作答。

易错点：局域守恒不等于每一点的密度都不变；也不要把末空只填“连续”。

适用条件：上述常规推导取实、局域势能及适当光滑性。δ势等奇异模型允许导数按匹配条件跳变，不能把课件的常规表述推广到所有奇异点。

Exam answer

Unchanged; the statistical interpretation of the wave function; the Schrödinger equation; continuous with a continuous first derivative.

Working

  1. For a normalized state $\rho=\Psi^*\Psi$. The Schrödinger equation for a real local potential and its complex conjugate yield $\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0$, where $\mathbf j=\hbar(\Psi^*\nabla\Psi-\Psi\nabla\Psi^*)/(2mi)$.
  2. Integration gives $d\int_\Omega\rho dV/dt=-\oint_{\partial\Omega}\mathbf j\cdot d\mathbf S$. Loss in a region is outward probability flux.
  3. With no net boundary flux, total probability stays constant, equal to one for a normalized state.
  4. The last blank follows the complete statement on slide PDF p. 51: continuity of the wave function and its first derivative.

Common error：Local conservation does not require time-independent density at each point. Do not omit the derivative condition in this particular slide-based question.

This regular-potential statement needs the stated smoothness and boundary assumptions. Singular potentials such as a delta potential have their own derivative jump conditions.

原卷 一、6 · PDF 1Source paper I.6 · PDF 1

势能为 $\frac12m\omega^2x^2$ 的一维线性谐振子的基态能量是（　　　）。

The ground-state energy of a one-dimensional oscillator with potential $m\omega^2x^2/2$ is (　　　).

提示Hint

注意最低能量与相邻能级间隔不是同一个量。

Distinguish the lowest energy from the level spacing.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$E_0=\frac12\hbar\omega$。

解析

  1. 不确定关系 $\Delta x\Delta p\ge\hbar/2$ 使动能与势能不能同时降为零。令 $y=(\Delta x)^2$，对中心均值为零的最低能态有 $E\ge\hbar^2/(8my)+m\omega^2y/2$。
  2. 右侧在 $y=\hbar/(2m\omega)$ 时取极小值 $\hbar\omega/2$；高斯基态能达到此下限。

易错点：$\hbar\omega$ 是相邻能级间隔；基态能量是它的一半。

Exam answer

$E_0=\hbar\omega/2$.

Working

  1. $\Delta x\Delta p\ge\hbar/2$ prevents both energy contributions from vanishing. For centered means, put $y=(\Delta x)^2$ to obtain $E\ge\hbar^2/(8my)+m\omega^2y/2$.
  2. The minimum occurs at $y=\hbar/(2m\omega)$ and equals $\hbar\omega/2$. A Gaussian ground state attains the bound.

Common error：$\hbar\omega$ is the level spacing, not the ground-state energy.

原卷 一、7 · PDF 1Source paper I.7 · PDF 1

微观粒子量子状态用波函数完全描述，波函数确定之后，粒子的任何一个力学量的（　　　）及其测量的可能值和相应的（　　　）也都被完全确定。

Once a wave function specifies the state, each observable’s (　　　), its possible measurement outcomes and their (　　　) are determined.

提示Hint

“分布被确定”与“一次结果被预先确定”不同。

A specified probability distribution does not predetermine every single outcome.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

平均值；几率分布（概率分布）。

解析

  1. 对归一化态 $\psi=\sum_n c_n\phi_n$，测得本征值 $f_n$ 的概率为 $|c_n|^2$；有简并时对同一本征值的分量求和。
  2. 平均值 $\langle F\rangle=\int\psi^*\hat F\psi\,dV=\sum_n f_n|c_n|^2$。因此态确定的是统计预言。
  3. 若不是 $\hat F$ 的本征态，单次测量仍可能得到不同允许值，并非所有力学量在该态都有确定值。

易错点：第二空优先写“几率分布”，贴合课件原句；不要误写“测量值全部确定”。

Exam answer

Expectation value; probability distribution.

Working

  1. For a normalized expansion $\psi=\sum_n c_n\phi_n$, the probability of $f_n$ is $|c_n|^2$; add weights within a degenerate eigenspace.
  2. $\langle F\rangle=\int\psi^*\hat F\psi dV=\sum_n f_n|c_n|^2$. The state determines statistical predictions.
  3. Unless it is an eigenstate of the observable, individual trials can still give different eigenvalues.

Common error：The determined object is the distribution, not a predetermined outcome for every observable.

一维体系中，坐标算符 $\hat x$ 与动量算符 $\hat p_x$ 的对易子 $[\hat x,\hat p_x]=$（　　　）。若两个力学量对应的厄米算符相互对易，则这两个力学量（　　　）同时具有确定的测量值（填“可以”或“不可以”）。

In one dimension, $[\hat x,\hat p_x]=$ (　　　). If two Hermitian observable operators commute, their observables (can / cannot) have definite values simultaneously.

提示Hint

第一空注意顺序；第二空说“可以”，并非“任意态都一定如此”。

Keep the commutator order; compatibility means that suitable common eigenstates can be chosen.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$i\hbar$；可以。

解析

  1. $\hat p_x=-i\hbar\partial_x$。作用于任意适当函数 $f$：$x\hat p_xf-\hat p_x(xf)=-i\hbar xf\prime+i\hbar(f+xf\prime)=i\hbar f$。
  2. 在本课程通常采用的定义域等条件下，对易的厄米算符可选取共同本征基；在共同本征态中两个量都有确定值。

易错点：“可以”不等于任意叠加态中的两个量都确定；也不能把 $[x,p_x]$ 的符号与 $[p_x,x]$ 混淆。

Exam answer

$i\hbar$; can.

Working

  1. Using $p_x=-i\hbar\partial_x$, $x p_xf-p_x(xf)=-i\hbar xf\prime+i\hbar(f+xf\prime)=i\hbar f$ for any suitable $f$.
  2. Under the usual domain assumptions in this course, commuting Hermitian operators admit a common eigenbasis. Their common eigenstates give definite values to both observables.

Common error：Compatibility does not say that both values are definite in every arbitrary state. Reversing the commutator changes its sign.

解析

  1. 在坐标表象，$\hat x f=xf$，$\hat p_x f=-i\hbar f\prime$。
  2. $\hat x\hat p_x f=-i\hbar xf\prime$；$\hat p_x\hat x f=-i\hbar(xf)\prime=-i\hbar(f+xf\prime)$。

“According to the uncertainty relation, physical observables cannot be measured accurately.” Is this correct? Explain.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

不对。不确定关系限制的是同一量子态中某些不对易量的统计分散度乘积，并不禁止单个力学量具有确定值或被准确测量。

解析

  1. $\Delta A=\sqrt{\langle A^2\rangle-\langle A\rangle^2}$ 是同样制备的一组体系的测量结果标准差，不是把仪器刻度做粗造成的误差。
  2. 一般 $\Delta A\Delta B\ge\frac12|\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle|$；对 $x,p_x$，右边恒为 $\hbar/2$，故二者不能在同一归一化物理态中都无涨落。
  3. 处于离散谱算符 $\hat A$ 的本征态时可有 $\Delta A=0$，其测量结果确定。例如定态能量可确定；不确定关系并未禁止这种情况。

易错点：不能笼统写成“所有力学量都不准”，也不能把一般非对易关系一概断言为每个态都有严格正下界。

Exam answer

No. The uncertainty relation bounds statistical spreads of certain observables in the same state; it does not forbid a sharp value or accurate measurement of one observable.

Working

  1. $\Delta A=\sqrt{\langle A^2\rangle-\langle A\rangle^2}$ is the standard deviation over identically prepared systems, not simply an instrument’s calibration error.
  2. $\Delta A\Delta B\ge|\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle|/2$. For $x,p_x$ the bound is always $\hbar/2$, precluding both spreads from vanishing in the same normalized physical state.
  3. An eigenstate of a discrete-spectrum observable can have $\Delta A=0$, such as definite energy in an energy eigenstate.

Common error：Do not conclude that every observable is intrinsically inaccurate. For a general nonzero commutator, its expectation and thus this lower bound can vanish in particular states.

  4. 归一化：$1=|A|^2\int_0^a\sin^2(n\pi x/a)dx=|A|^2a/2$，取实正系数 $A=\sqrt{2/a}$。
  5. 基态密度为 $\rho_1=(2/a)\sin^2(\pi x/a)$。$\langle x\rangle_1=(2/a)\int_0^a x\sin^2(\pi x/a)dx$。
  6. 用 $\sin^2u=(1-\cos2u)/2$：$\langle x\rangle_1=\frac1a\int_0^a xdx-\frac1a\int_0^a x\cos(2\pi x/a)dx$。前一项为 $a/2$；后一积分的原函数为 $x\sin bx/b+\cos bx/b^2$（$b=2\pi/a$），两端差为0。
  7. 故 $\langle x\rangle_1=a/2$。也可用密度关于 $a/2$ 对称验证：平均位置在阱中心，但不表示粒子始终停在中心。

易错点：阱在 $[0,a]$，位置平均值不是0；不能套用中心对称阱 $[-a,a]$ 的坐标结果或半宽能量式。

Exam answer

$E_n=n^2\pi^2\hbar^2/(2m_ea^2)$, $n=1,2,\ldots$. Inside, $\psi_n=\sqrt{2/a}\sin(n\pi x/a)$; outside it is zero. A time-dependent energy eigenstate is $\Psi_n=\psi_n e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}$. The ground-state mean is $\langle x\rangle_1=a/2$.

Working

  1. The source explicitly gives only the exterior potential. Use the standard convention $V=0$ inside, $a>0$, and electron mass $m_e$. Then $-\hbar^2\psi\prime\prime/(2m_e)=E\psi$.
  2. Put $k=\sqrt{2m_eE}/\hbar$ and write $\psi=A\sin kx+B\cos kx$. Infinite walls impose $\psi(0)=\psi(a)=0$, giving $B=0$ and $ka=n\pi$.
  3. $n=0$ is the zero function and cannot be normalized; negative $n$ only changes an overall sign. Choose positive integers and obtain the stated energies.
  4. $1=|A|^2\int_0^a\sin^2(n\pi x/a)dx=|A|^2a/2$, hence a positive real amplitude is $\sqrt{2/a}$.
  5. $\rho_1=(2/a)\sin^2(\pi x/a)$ and $\langle x\rangle_1=(2/a)\int_0^a x\sin^2(\pi x/a)dx$.
  6. Using the double-angle identity gives $a^{-1}\int_0^a xdx-a^{-1}\int_0^a x\cos(2\pi x/a)dx$. The first term is $a/2$; the second integral is zero by its endpoint values.
  7. Thus $\langle x\rangle_1=a/2$, also verified by reflection symmetry about the midpoint. A mean at the center does not mean the electron remains there.

Common error：The well is $[0,a]$, not $[-a,a]$. Do not import a zero position mean or a half-width energy formula.
