Compton 效应证实了光具有（　　　）、Davisson 和 Germer 的实验证实了电子具有（　　　）。

The Compton effect confirms the (　　　) of light; the Davisson–Germer experiment confirms the (　　　) of electrons.

提示Hint

区分“光子碰撞中交换能量和动量”与“晶体衍射”。

Distinguish photon collision kinematics from diffraction by a crystal.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

粒子性；波动性。

解析

  1. 康普顿效应可按光子与电子碰撞处理，光子携带 $E=h\nu$、$p=h/\lambda$；这体现光的粒子性。
  2. Davisson–Germer 实验中电子束经晶体散射形成衍射极大，验证电子的波动性及 $\lambda=h/p$。

易错点：不要把两个实验的证据方向颠倒。实验分别突出某一方面，不表示光或电子只具有这一种性质。

Exam answer

Particle character; wave character.

Working

  1. Compton scattering is described using photon energy $E=h\nu$ and momentum $p=h/\lambda$ exchanged with an electron, demonstrating the particle aspect of light.
  2. Electron diffraction from a crystal in Davisson–Germer verifies the wave aspect and $\lambda=h/p$.

Common error：The two experiments emphasize different aspects; neither says the object has only that aspect.

Give the three regularity properties in the slides, rather than a probability integral.

可直接作答

连续性、有限性、单值性（顺序可调）。

解析

Continuous, finite, and single-valued (any order).

  2. Normalization of a square-integrable state is a separate condition, $\int |\Psi|^2dV=1$.

Common error：Do not replace one regularity condition with normalization. Singular potentials require model-specific matching conditions.

对易式 $[\hat F,\hat G]$ 等于（　　　）。

The commutator $[\hat F,\hat G]$ equals (　　　).

提示Hint

写出两种作用次序的差，注意先后顺序。

Subtract the products in opposite orders, retaining their order.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$[\hat F,\hat G]=\hat F\hat G-\hat G\hat F$。

解析

  1. $\hat F\hat G\psi$ 的意思是先让 $\hat G$ 作用，再让 $\hat F$ 作用。
  2. 若对定义域内的函数两种次序作用相同，即 $[\hat F,\hat G]=0$，称为对易；否则称为不对易。

易错点：$[\hat G,\hat F]=-[\hat F,\hat G]$，所以反写次序会改变符号。

Exam answer

$[\hat F,\hat G]=\hat F\hat G-\hat G\hat F$.

Working

  1. In $\hat F\hat G\psi$, $\hat G$ acts first. The commutator compares this with the reverse order.
  2. If the difference vanishes on the appropriate domain, the operators commute.

Common error：Reversing the labels reverses the sign: $[\hat G,\hat F]=-[\hat F,\hat G]$.

$\nu=E/h$.

Working

Common error：$E/\hbar$ is angular frequency, not ordinary frequency. Use the energy convention specified by the model.

几率守恒具有局域性质，当空间某处几率减少了，必然另外一些地方几率增加，使总几率（　　　），并伴随着某种流来实现这种变化。几率守恒定律是（　　　）和（　　　）的推论，并不是量子力学中的一条独立假设。由几率密度和几率流密度的表达式中可知，波函数在空间坐标的变化全部区域内应是（　　　）。

It follows from (　　　) and (　　　), rather than being an independent postulate. From the density and current expressions, the wave function throughout the spatial domain should be (　　　).

提示Hint

最后一空应把课件第51页的整项要求写全，而不只写三个标准条件中的一个词。

For the last blank, include the full requirement on slide PDF p. 51, rather than just one of the three regularity words.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

不变；波函数的统计解释；薛定谔方程；连续的，且有连续的微商。

  1. 按统计解释，归一化态的密度 $\rho=\Psi^*\Psi$。对实势能的薛定谔方程及其复共轭式作差，可得 $\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0$，其中 $\mathbf j=\frac{\hbar}{2mi}(\Psi^*\nabla\Psi-\Psi\nabla\Psi^*)$。

  3. 在全空间无净流出、或适当封闭边界条件下，总概率不随时间变化；已归一化时恒为1。
  4. 课件第51页把最后一项写作“连续的，且有连续的微商”，本题按这项完整作答。

Unchanged; the statistical interpretation of the wave function; the Schrödinger equation; continuous with a continuous first derivative.

  3. With no net boundary flux, total probability stays constant, equal to one for a normalized state.
  4. The last blank follows the complete statement on slide PDF p. 51: continuity of the wave function and its first derivative.

势能为 $\frac12m\omega^2x^2$ 的一维线性谐振子的基态能量是（　　　）。

The ground-state energy of a one-dimensional oscillator with potential $m\omega^2x^2/2$ is (　　　).

提示Hint

注意最低能量与相邻能级间隔不是同一个量。

Distinguish the lowest energy from the level spacing.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

$E_0=\frac12\hbar\omega$。

解析

  1. 不确定关系 $\Delta x\Delta p\ge\hbar/2$ 使动能与势能不能同时降为零。令 $y=(\Delta x)^2$，对中心均值为零的最低能态有 $E\ge\hbar^2/(8my)+m\omega^2y/2$。
  2. 右侧在 $y=\hbar/(2m\omega)$ 时取极小值 $\hbar\omega/2$；高斯基态能达到此下限。

易错点：$\hbar\omega$ 是相邻能级间隔；基态能量是它的一半。

Exam answer

$E_0=\hbar\omega/2$.

Working

  1. $\Delta x\Delta p\ge\hbar/2$ prevents both energy contributions from vanishing. For centered means, put $y=(\Delta x)^2$ to obtain $E\ge\hbar^2/(8my)+m\omega^2y/2$.
  2. The minimum occurs at $y=\hbar/(2m\omega)$ and equals $\hbar\omega/2$. A Gaussian ground state attains the bound.

Common error：$\hbar\omega$ is the level spacing, not the ground-state energy.

微观粒子量子状态用波函数完全描述，波函数确定之后，粒子的任何一个力学量的（　　　）及其测量的可能值和相应的（　　　）也都被完全确定。

Once a wave function specifies the state, each observable’s (　　　), its possible measurement outcomes and their (　　　) are determined.

提示Hint

“分布被确定”与“一次结果被预先确定”不同。

A specified probability distribution does not predetermine every single outcome.

参考答案与完整解析Answer & full working

可直接作答

平均值；几率分布（概率分布）。

解析

  1. 对归一化态 $\psi=\sum_n c_n\phi_n$，测得本征值 $f_n$ 的概率为 $|c_n|^2$；有简并时对同一本征值的分量求和。
  2. 平均值 $\langle F\rangle=\int\psi^*\hat F\psi\,dV=\sum_n f_n|c_n|^2$。因此态确定的是统计预言。
  3. 若不是 $\hat F$ 的本征态，单次测量仍可能得到不同允许值，并非所有力学量在该态都有确定值。

易错点：第二空优先写“几率分布”，贴合课件原句；不要误写“测量值全部确定”。

Exam answer

Expectation value; probability distribution.

Working

  1. For a normalized expansion $\psi=\sum_n c_n\phi_n$, the probability of $f_n$ is $|c_n|^2$; add weights within a degenerate eigenspace.
  2. $\langle F\rangle=\int\psi^*\hat F\psi dV=\sum_n f_n|c_n|^2$. The state determines statistical predictions.
  3. Unless it is an eigenstate of the observable, individual trials can still give different eigenvalues.

Common error：The determined object is the distribution, not a predetermined outcome for every observable.

阱外势垒无限高，电子不可能出现在那里，所以阱外 $\psi=0$；无限深阱的边界条件为

$$\psi(0)=0,\qquad \psi(a)=0.$$

这两个条件是本题的关键：阱内能出现的波形必须同时满足左右两端为零。注意，边界条件要求的是波函数为零，并没有要求它的导数也为零。

第2步：写出阱内定态薛定谔方程

一维定态方程的一般形式是

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e}\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2}+V(x)\psi=E\psi.$$

阱内 $V=0$，代入后移项，并乘以 $-2m_e/\hbar^2$：

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e}\psi''=E\psi,$$ $$\psi''+\frac{2m_eE}{\hbar^2}\psi=0.$$

阱内总能量就是动能，不能为负；如果 $E=0$，方程变成 $\psi''=0$，解为 $Cx+D$，两端为零又使 $C=D=0$，这不是能归一化的物理态。因此取 $E>0$，令

$$k^2=\frac{2m_eE}{\hbar^2},\qquad k=\frac{\sqrt{2m_eE}}{\hbar}>0.$$

方程就变成 $\psi''+k^2\psi=0$。因为 $\sin kx$ 和 $\cos kx$ 各求两次导数都会变成原函数乘以 $-k^2$，所以通解为

$$\psi(x)=A\sin kx+B\cos kx.$$

此时 $A$、$B$、$k$ 还没有确定；接下来用边界条件和归一化条件来确定。

第3步：逐个代入左右边界，得到允许的波数

左壁：代入 $x=0$，利用 $\sin0=0$、$\cos0=1$：

$$0=\psi(0)=A\cdot0+B\cdot1=B.$$

所以 $B=0$，剩下 $\psi(x)=A\sin kx$。

右壁：再代入 $x=a$：

$$0=\psi(a)=A\sin ka.$$

不能取 $A=0$，否则波函数处处为零，总概率也为零。因此必须有 $\sin ka=0$。正弦函数在整数倍的 $\pi$ 处为零，于是

$$ka=n\pi,\qquad k_n=\frac{n\pi}{a},\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.$$

$n=0$ 对应上面已经排除的零能量零函数；负整数只会使正弦波整体变号，不产生新的能级或概率密度，所以用正整数编号即可。直观上，阱宽内必须刚好容纳整数个半波长，这就是能量出现离散取值的原因。

第4步：从波数换算成能量本征值

由第2步的定义先解出 $E$，再代入第3步的 $k_n$：

$$E=\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m_e},$$ $$E_n=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e}\left(\frac{n\pi}{a}\right)^2 =\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2m_ea^2},\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.$$

特别地，基态是最低能级 $n=1$，所以 $E_1=\pi^2\hbar^2/(2m_ea^2)$。此处 $a$ 是整个阱宽，能量分母中的 $a^2$ 不能随意换成其他长度。

第5步：把归一化积分完整算出来，确定 $A$

目前只知道波形 $\psi_n=A\sin(n\pi x/a)$。波函数模平方是概率密度；电子一定在阱内，所以阱内总概率必须为1：

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\psi_n(x)|^2\,dx =|A|^2\int_0^a\sin^2\left(\frac{n\pi x}{a}\right)dx=1.$$

为什么积分限变为 $0$ 到 $a$？因为阱外波函数为零，对积分没有贡献。先单独算其中的正弦平方积分，用恒等式 $\sin^2u=(1-\cos2u)/2$：

$$\int_0^a\sin^2\left(\frac{n\pi x}{a}\right)dx =\frac12\int_0^a\left[1-\cos\left(\frac{2n\pi x}{a}\right)\right]dx,$$ $$=\left[\frac{x}{2}-\frac{a}{4n\pi}\sin\left(\frac{2n\pi x}{a}\right)\right]_0^a =\frac a2.$$

最后一步用了 $\sin(2n\pi)=\sin0=0$。代回归一化条件：

$$|A|^2\frac a2=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |A|^2=\frac2a.$$

归一化只确定 $A$ 的模；整体相位不影响概率，因此通常选取实数正值 $A=\sqrt{2/a}$。

第6步：写完整的归一化波函数，完成第（1）问

不能只写阱内表达式，还要交代阱外为零：

$$\psi_n(x)=\begin{cases} \sqrt{\frac2a}\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{a}\right),&0<x<a,\\ 0,&x\le0\ \text{或}\ x\ge a, \end{cases}\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.$$

若题目把“波函数”理解为含时的定态波函数，还可以补上时间因子：

$$\Psi_n(x,t)=\psi_n(x)e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}.$$

这里 $i$ 是虚数单位。时间因子的模为1，所以它不会改变概率密度：$|\Psi_n|^2=|\psi_n|^2$。

第7步：代入 $n=1$，写出位置期望值的积分

基态的空间波函数与概率密度分别是

$$\psi_1(x)=\sqrt{\frac2a}\sin\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right),\qquad \rho_1(x)=|\psi_1(x)|^2=\frac2a\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right).$$

“位置期望值”就是对大量同样制备的电子反复测量位置后得到的平均值。连续分布的平均值等于“位置乘以该处的概率密度，再对所有位置积分”：

$$\langle x\rangle_1=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi_1^*(x)\,x\,\psi_1(x)\,dx =\int_0^a x\rho_1(x)\,dx,$$ $$\langle x\rangle_1=\frac2a\int_0^a x\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right)dx.$$

星号 $*$ 表示复共轭；本题的 $\psi_1$ 是实函数，因此 $\psi_1^*=\psi_1$。注意期望值的积分里比归一化积分多了一个 $x$。

第8步：用二倍角公式拆成两个容易计算的积分

再次使用 $\sin^2u=(1-\cos2u)/2$：

$$\langle x\rangle_1=\frac1a\int_0^a x\,dx -\frac1a\int_0^a x\cos\left(\frac{2\pi x}{a}\right)dx.$$

第一项：直接积分：

$$\frac1a\int_0^a x\,dx=\frac1a\left[\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^a=\frac a2.$$

第二项：先记 $b=2\pi/a$，并令 $I=\int_0^a x\cos(bx)\,dx$。使用分部积分公式 $\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$，选择

$$u=x,\quad du=dx,\qquad dv=\cos(bx)dx,\quad v=\frac{\sin(bx)}b.$$

逐步代入；注意 $\int\sin(bx)dx=-\cos(bx)/b$，因此最后是加号：

$$I=\left[\frac{x\sin(bx)}b\right]_0^a-\frac1b\int_0^a\sin(bx)dx,$$ $$I=\left[\frac{x\sin(bx)}b+\frac{\cos(bx)}{b^2}\right]_0^a.$$

代入 $ba=2\pi$，用 $\sin2\pi=0$、$\cos2\pi=\cos0=1$，把上、下限分别写出：

$$I=\left(\frac{a\sin2\pi}b+\frac{\cos2\pi}{b^2}\right) -\left(0+\frac{\cos0}{b^2}\right) =\frac1{b^2}-\frac1{b^2}=0.$$

所以第（2）问得到

$$\boxed{\langle x\rangle_1=\frac a2-\frac Ia=\frac a2}.$$

第9步：用对称性检查答案，并理解结果

基态密度满足 $\rho_1(a-x)=\rho_1(x)$，也就是关于阱中心 $a/2$ 左右对称。把相对中心的位置记作 $y=x-a/2$，左右两侧的 $y\rho_1$ 在积分时正负抵消，因此 $\langle y\rangle=0$、$\langle x\rangle=a/2$，与上面的计算一致。

这个结果表示平均位置在中心，不表示每次测量都得到中心，也不表示电子静止在中心。检查量纲也能发现问题：$\sqrt{2/a}$ 的量纲应为长度的负二分之一次方，$\langle x\rangle$ 应具有长度量纲。

学会以后，考场上怎样压缩书写？

第（1）问保留“定态方程与边界 → $k_na=n\pi$ → $E_n$ → 归一化积分 → 完整 $\psi_n$”；第（2）问保留“代入基态 → 写期望值积分 → 算出两个积分 → $a/2$”。学习时看完整推导，答题时仍要留下这些得分依据，不能只写最后结果。

易错点：阱是 $[0,a]$，平均位置不是0；$n$ 从1开始；归一化积分用 $|\psi|^2$，期望值还要乘 $x$；波函数要包含系数 $\sqrt{2/a}$ 和阱外为0的说明；三角函数积分中的 $\pi$、$a$ 和分部积分的符号不能省略。

练习用参考分配：第（1）问方程与边界4分、能级4分、归一化波函数4分；第（2）问积分设置3分、计算或对称性论证4分、结果1分。这不是学校公布的评分细则。

Read the problem: what must be found?

There are two tasks: (1) the allowed electron energies and their wave functions; (2) the ground-state position expectation value. The source explicitly states an infinite exterior potential. Following the standard slide model, take $V=0$ inside, well width $a>0$, and electron mass $m_e$.

Symbols: $x$ is measured from the left wall; $a$ is the full distance between the walls; $\hbar=h/(2\pi)$. The spatial wave function is $\psi_n(x)$ and the time-dependent function is $\Psi_n(x,t)$. The positive integer $n$ labels an energy level. An energy eigenvalue $E_n$ is an allowed definite energy in this model.

Route: write the interior equation, apply both walls to select the wave numbers, obtain the energies, normalize, and finally calculate the ground-state position mean. Each step is worked out below.

Step 1: identify the interior, exterior and boundaries

The infinite exterior barriers prevent the electron from appearing outside, so the exterior wave function is zero. The infinite-well boundary conditions are

$$\psi(0)=0,\qquad\psi(a)=0.$$

Every allowed interior wave must vanish at both ends. These conditions set the function to zero; they do not also set its derivative to zero.

Step 2: write the stationary equation inside the well

Start with the one-dimensional time-independent Schrödinger equation:

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e}\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2}+V(x)\psi=E\psi.$$

Put $V=0$ inside, rearrange and multiply by $-2m_e/\hbar^2$:

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e}\psi''=E\psi,$$ $$\psi''+\frac{2m_eE}{\hbar^2}\psi=0.$$

The interior energy is kinetic energy and cannot be negative. If $E=0$, the equation gives $\psi=Cx+D$; both zero endpoint values force $C=D=0$, which cannot be normalized. Thus use $E>0$ and define

$$k^2=\frac{2m_eE}{\hbar^2},\qquad k=\frac{\sqrt{2m_eE}}{\hbar}>0.$$

The equation becomes $\psi''+k^2\psi=0$. Both sine and cosine have second derivatives equal to $-k^2$ times themselves, giving the general solution

$$\psi(x)=A\sin kx+B\cos kx.$$

The constants $A$, $B$ and $k$ remain to be fixed by the walls and normalization.

Step 3: apply each wall to obtain allowed wave numbers

Left wall: substitute $x=0$, using $\sin0=0$ and $\cos0=1$:

$$0=\psi(0)=A\cdot0+B\cdot1=B.$$

Hence $B=0$ and $\psi=A\sin kx$.

Right wall: substitute $x=a$:

$$0=\psi(a)=A\sin ka.$$

Choosing $A=0$ would make the whole function zero, with total probability zero. We therefore require $\sin ka=0$. Sine vanishes at integer multiples of $\pi$, so

$$ka=n\pi,\qquad k_n=\frac{n\pi}{a},\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.$$

The zero-energy case $n=0$ was excluded above. A negative integer only changes the overall sign of the sine, giving no new energy or density. Positive integers suffice. Physically, an integer number of half wavelengths must fit between the walls; this selects discrete energies.

Step 4: turn the wave numbers into energy eigenvalues

First solve the definition of $k$ for $E$, then substitute $k_n$:

$$E=\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m_e},$$ $$E_n=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e}\left(\frac{n\pi}{a}\right)^2 =\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2m_ea^2},\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.$$

The ground state is the lowest level, $n=1$, with $E_1=\pi^2\hbar^2/(2m_ea^2)$. Here $a$ means the full well width.

Step 5: evaluate the normalization integral to find $A$

So far the shape is $\psi_n=A\sin(n\pi x/a)$. Its modulus squared is probability density. The electron must be somewhere in the well, so

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\psi_n(x)|^2\,dx =|A|^2\int_0^a\sin^2\left(\frac{n\pi x}{a}\right)dx=1.$$

The limits reduce to $0$ and $a$ because the exterior function is zero. Evaluate the sine-squared integral using $\sin^2u=(1-\cos2u)/2$:

$$\int_0^a\sin^2\left(\frac{n\pi x}{a}\right)dx =\frac12\int_0^a\left[1-\cos\left(\frac{2n\pi x}{a}\right)\right]dx,$$ $$=\left[\frac{x}{2}-\frac{a}{4n\pi}\sin\left(\frac{2n\pi x}{a}\right)\right]_0^a=\frac a2.$$

The endpoint sine terms vanish because $\sin(2n\pi)=\sin0=0$. Substitution gives

$$|A|^2\frac a2=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |A|^2=\frac2a.$$

Normalization fixes the magnitude of $A$. An overall phase does not affect probability, so choose the positive real value $A=\sqrt{2/a}$.

Step 6: state the full normalized functions for part (1)

Include the zero exterior, as well as the interior formula:

$$\psi_n(x)=\begin{cases} \sqrt{\frac2a}\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{a}\right),&0<x<a,\\ 0,&x\le0\ \text{or}\ x\ge a, \end{cases}\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.$$

If a time-dependent stationary-state wave function is intended, also give

$$\Psi_n(x,t)=\psi_n(x)e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}.$$

Here $i$ is the imaginary unit. The time factor has modulus one, so $|\Psi_n|^2=|\psi_n|^2$.

Step 7: set $n=1$ and construct the position-mean integral

The ground-state spatial function and density are

$$\psi_1(x)=\sqrt{\frac2a}\sin\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right),\qquad \rho_1(x)=|\psi_1(x)|^2=\frac2a\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right).$$

The position expectation value is the average of repeated position measurements on identically prepared electrons. For a continuous distribution, multiply each position by its probability density and integrate:

$$\langle x\rangle_1=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\psi_1^*(x)\,x\,\psi_1(x)\,dx =\int_0^a x\rho_1(x)\,dx,$$ $$\langle x\rangle_1=\frac2a\int_0^a x\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi x}{a}\right)dx.$$

The star means complex conjugation. This $\psi_1$ is real, so $\psi_1^*=\psi_1$. Compared with normalization, the expectation integral contains an extra factor $x$.

Step 8: split and evaluate both integrals

Use $\sin^2u=(1-\cos2u)/2$ again:

$$\langle x\rangle_1=\frac1a\int_0^a x\,dx -\frac1a\int_0^a x\cos\left(\frac{2\pi x}{a}\right)dx.$$

First term:

$$\frac1a\int_0^a x\,dx=\frac1a\left[\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^a=\frac a2.$$

Second term: set $b=2\pi/a$ and $I=\int_0^a x\cos(bx)\,dx$. Use integration by parts, $\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$, with

$$u=x,\quad du=dx,\qquad dv=\cos(bx)dx,\quad v=\frac{\sin(bx)}b.$$

Substitute step by step. Since $\int\sin(bx)dx=-\cos(bx)/b$, the final cosine term has a plus sign:

$$I=\left[\frac{x\sin(bx)}b\right]_0^a-\frac1b\int_0^a\sin(bx)dx,$$ $$I=\left[\frac{x\sin(bx)}b+\frac{\cos(bx)}{b^2}\right]_0^a.$$

Now $ba=2\pi$, $\sin2\pi=0$ and $\cos2\pi=\cos0=1$. Write out both endpoint values:

$$I=\left(\frac{a\sin2\pi}b+\frac{\cos2\pi}{b^2}\right) -\left(0+\frac{\cos0}{b^2}\right) =\frac1{b^2}-\frac1{b^2}=0.$$

Therefore part (2) gives

$$\boxed{\langle x\rangle_1=\frac a2-\frac Ia=\frac a2}.$$

Step 9: check by symmetry and interpret the answer

The density satisfies $\rho_1(a-x)=\rho_1(x)$ and is symmetric about $a/2$. With $y=x-a/2$, contributions to the mean of $y$ cancel between the two sides. Thus $\langle y\rangle=0$ and $\langle x\rangle=a/2$, agreeing with the calculation.

A mean at the center does not mean every measurement returns the center, or that the electron stays there. Check units too: $\sqrt{2/a}$ has units of inverse square root of length, and $\langle x\rangle$ has units of length.

How to shorten this working in an exam

For part (1), retain the equation and boundaries, $k_na=n\pi$, the energy formula, normalization integral, and complete $\psi_n$. For part (2), retain the ground-state substitution, expectation integral, both evaluated terms, and $a/2$. Study the full derivation first, then compress it while keeping the reasons that support each result.

Common errors: the interval is $[0,a]$, so the mean is not zero; $n$ starts at 1; normalize $|\psi|^2$ and include an extra $x$ for the position mean; retain $\sqrt{2/a}$ and the zero exterior; keep the factors of $\pi$ and $a$ and the signs in integration by parts.

Suggested practice marking: part (1), equation/boundaries 4, energies 4, normalized functions 4; part (2), integral setup 3, calculation or symmetry proof 4, result 1. These are not official marking instructions.
